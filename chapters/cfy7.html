Lead

Some addresses appear twice for a reason.

Mystery Tension Dark By AKA

Sato — POV

Sato picked up his coat and quietly slipped the apartment key into his pocket.

He headed toward the office door.

"Akane."

She looked up from her desk.

"I'll be back soon."

Akane frowned.

"Where are you going?"

Sato hesitated.

"Somewhere important."

Akane narrowed her eyes.

"...Is this about Sengoku?"

Sato remained silent.

His silence was enough.

Akane sighed.

"So it is about Sengoku."

She stood up from her chair.

"Then I'm coming with you."

Sato immediately shook his head.

"No."

"...Why not?"

"I don't know what we'll find there."

Akane crossed her arms.

"Find where?"

Sato finally answered.

"...Sengoku's apartment."

For a moment, Akane was speechless.

"You found the address?"

"It was hidden inside the old case files."

Akane stepped closer.

"Sato... you're not going there alone."

Before Sato could respond—

The office door suddenly burst open.

An officer rushed inside, breathing heavily.

"Excuse me!"

Both detectives turned toward him.

"Are you the homicide detectives assigned to the serial murder case?"

Sato nodded.

"Yes."

"We've just received another murder report."

The room instantly became quiet.

Sato's expression hardened.

"Location?"

The officer looked down at his notebook.

"Street 420, East Highway."

Sato froze.

"We'll be there in a few minutes."

He grabbed his coat.

"Akane."

She nodded.

"Let's go."

Breaking News

A well-known cruise captain, Casper Voss, who served in the military for over twenty-five years, has been found dead inside his apartment. Authorities have begun an investigation, but no official statement has been released regarding the cause of death.

Inside the Police Car

Inside the police car, neither detective spoke.

Until...

Akane noticed Sato staring silently out the window.

"You look shocked."

Sato slowly turned toward her.

His voice was barely above a whisper.

"Akane..."

"The address we just received..."

He pulled the old case file from his bag.

📁 Old Case File · Address on Record
— Street 420
— Apartment 4
— Room 102
— East Highway

Sato slowly closed the file.

"...It's the same apartment."

Akane frowned.

"What do you mean?"

Sato looked straight ahead.

"It's where Sengoku lived."

The police car accelerated into the night.

Neither of them realized...

Someone had already reached the apartment before they did.
