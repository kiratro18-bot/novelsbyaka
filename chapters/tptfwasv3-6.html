What a drag…

Noa told me to wait near the sakura tree after class.

I didn't ask why.

I probably already knew.

I sat down beneath the tree, petals drifting slowly above me.

Why does everything come back to this place?

Sakura Tree University Campus

"…Um. Hello, Haruki."

That voice.

I looked up.

"Sora?"

She stood there, hands clenched, like she was holding herself together.

"Haruki, please listen to me."

"I don't—"

"Please," she said softly. "Just once."

I exhaled. "…Say it."

She swallowed.

"That day… when you confessed," she began, voice shaking, "I indirectly said yes."

My fingers tightened.

"But soon after, I transferred," she continued. "Without telling you anything. I ran away."

She looked down.

"I'm sorry. I know I don't have the right to ask for forgiveness."

Then, barely above a whisper—

"The truth is… I wanted to stay. Really badly. With you."

Silence.

The sakura petals kept falling, like nothing heavy had just been said.

🌸 Haruki — Replies

"Don't worry, Sora. I don't hate you for that.
I've… moved on."

"Oh…" she whispered.

Her shoulders dropped.

I looked away.

Have I really moved on?

Or did I just bury it deep enough to pretend?

True Feelings

No running. No hesitation.

POV: Noa & Ren Decision By AKA

POV: Noa

My room was dark, phone face-down on the bed.

I turned to my side. Then the other.

Sleep refused to come.

Today wasn't supposed to happen like this.

Haruki's face kept appearing in my head — the way he avoided Sora, the way his voice stayed calm but distant. Too calm.

Do I like him?

No… that wasn't the right question anymore.

I do like him.

The real question was worse.

Does he feel the same?

And Sora…

What was she trying to do?

Was she just apologizing to clear her regret… or trying to take him back?

My chest tightened.

🌸 Noa — Remembers

The cafeteria. The voices.
The conversation I wasn't supposed to hear.

I shouldn't have listened.

But I did.

She apologized to Ren.

She apologized to Haruki.

So where did that leave me?

I stared at the ceiling.

The summer festival flashed in my mind — lights, music, fireworks bursting open the night sky.

Two months.

I made a decision.

I'll confess to Haruki at the fireworks.

No running.

No hesitation.

If he says yes — good.

If he doesn't…

At least I'll know.

💭 Noa

No running.
No hesitation.

"Oh. Hi, Sora."

"Hi, Ren… I was just lost in thought."

She looked smaller somehow. Quieter.

I wanted to ask.

Who was that boy?

The one under the sakura tree.

Is he someone special to you?

But the words stuck.

"Sora—"

"Ren, Ren," she said softly, pulling me back.

"Yeah?"

She hesitated, fingers curling around her bag strap.

"Can we… be friends again?"

That simple sentence hit harder than any confession.

Friends.

I forced a smile.

"Sure."

I said it out loud.

But inside, something twisted.

Friends… huh.

Was that all I was allowed to be now?
