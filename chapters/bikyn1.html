Chapter 01
Before I Forget Your Name

The Seat Beside the Window

Some ordinary Mondays don't stay ordinary.

POV: Hina Transfer Student Comedy By AKA

There are two kinds of students in every classroom.

The ones who pay attention.

And the ones who spend the first five minutes of every lesson wondering what they'll eat for lunch.

Unfortunately...

I belonged to the second group.

"...Hina."

Our homeroom teacher's voice cut through my thoughts.

"Hm?"

"Would you like to tell the class the answer?"

"...If I say 'bread,' can we pretend I was listening?"

The room burst into laughter. Even our teacher pinched the bridge of his nose.

"You were thinking about food again, weren't you?"

"...Maybe."

"Stand."

I let out the most dramatic sigh my body could produce before standing.

The punishment wasn't serious.

Just five seconds of public embarrassment.

Still...

It was five seconds too many.

"Sit down."

"Thank you for your mercy."

A few classmates chuckled as I dropped back into my seat.

Mission accomplished.

If I was going to get scolded, I might as well make everyone laugh.

The classroom settled once again.

Sunlight slipped through the windows, warming the wooden floor.

Outside, the baseball club could already be heard shouting from the field, even though classes weren't over yet.

A perfectly ordinary Monday.

Exactly the kind of day that disappears from your memory after a week.

this one didn't.

Our teacher checked the attendance sheet one last time before looking toward the door.

"Oh, right."

He smiled.

"We have someone new joining us today."

The classroom instantly came alive.

A transfer student?

In the middle of the semester?

Now this was more interesting than math.

The classroom door slid open.

A boy stepped inside.

He wore the same uniform as the rest of us, though it looked almost too neat, as if he'd spent extra time making sure everything was in place.

His black hair was slightly messy despite that effort.

Not in a fashionable way.

Just... naturally.

His eyes quietly wandered across the classroom, taking in unfamiliar faces.

He didn't seem nervous.

He seemed...

Careful.

"This is Haruto Kisaragi," the teacher said.

new name to remember.

"He transferred here over the weekend."

Haruto bowed.

"My name is Haruto Kisaragi."

His voice was calm.

"I hope we can get along."

No dramatic speech.

No attempt to impress anyone.

Just one polite sentence.

The teacher pointed toward the only empty desk.

"The seat beside the window."

Lucky.

Every student secretly wanted that seat.

Fresh air.

A nice view.

And the perfect excuse to stare outside whenever class became boring.

Haruto thanked the teacher quietly before taking his seat.

For the rest of the lesson, everyone pretended to pay attention.

Nobody actually did.

Every few minutes, someone stole another glance at the new guy.

Including me.

"..."

Okay.

Maybe more than a few.

· · ·
Lunch bell

Lunch break arrived like a prison sentence finally ending.

The moment the bell rang, chairs scraped against the floor.

Within seconds, Haruto's desk was surrounded.

"So where did you transfer from?" "No."
"Do you play basketball?" "Sometimes."
"Do you have a girlfriend?" "Not really."
"What games do you play?" "I mostly read."

Question after question.

The poor guy answered every one politely.

He never sounded annoyed.

But after ten minutes...

Even I was getting socially exhausted just watching him.

Eventually, the crowd drifted away in search of food.

Haruto let out the smallest sigh.

"...Survived?"

The word slipped out before I could stop it.

"...Barely."

He looked up. I laughed.

"So that's your first boss battle at this school."

"I wasn't prepared for it."

"You'll level up."

"I hope so."

"...Those were actually decent jokes."

"I practiced."

"You practiced making jokes?"

"I had a long train ride."

"...You know what?"

"What?"

"I believe you."

For the first time... he laughed. It wasn't loud. It wasn't exaggerated. Just a quiet laugh that reached his eyes. Somehow, it made the classroom feel less unfamiliar.

I walked over with my lunch.

"Mind if I sit here?"

Haruto glanced at the empty seats around us.

"I don't think anyone else was planning to."

"...Was that another joke?"

"...I think so."

"They're getting better."

"I'll take that as a compliment."

"You should."

I placed my lunch on the desk and held out my hand.

"I'm Hina Aizawa."

He shook it.

"Haruto Kisaragi."

properly introduced, this time.

His handshake was warm.

Not awkward.

Not overly confident.

Just...

Comfortable.

For a while, we simply ate. It wasn't an uncomfortable silence. Some people force conversations because silence scares them. This wasn't like that. The silence itself felt... easy.

After finishing the last bite of my sandwich, I stood.

"Well..."

"I'll see you tomorrow."

Haruto looked up.

"...See you tomorrow, Hina."

I smiled without thinking.

"Good."

"...You remembered my name."

He tilted his head.

"I'd hope so."

"I only learned it today."

"...Fair enough."

I laughed, waved goodbye, and walked out of the classroom.

End of Chapter 01
For some reason, the rest of the day felt a little brighter than usual.
