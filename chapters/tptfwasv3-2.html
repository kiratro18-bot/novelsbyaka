I Made a Friend… Did I?

The kind of collision you don't regret.

POV: Sora Connection New Faces By AKA

I was late. Again.

The psychology building stood ahead of me like it was judging my life choices, and I quickened my pace, clutching my notebook to my chest.

"Sorry—!"

The word barely left my mouth before I collided with someone. Books slipped from our hands, pages scattering across the ground.

"I'm so sorry," I said quickly, crouching down. "Are you hurt?"

"No, no," the girl replied just as fast. "I should be the one apologizing. I wasn't looking where I was going."

We both laughed awkwardly as we picked up our things.

She had bright eyes, the kind that looked like they noticed everything even when pretending not to. As we stood up, she glanced at the building behind me.

"Are you heading to psychology class?" she asked.

"Yes," I answered, a little relieved. "I thought I was the only one running late."

"Perfect," she said with a smile. "Let's go together then?"

"Oh—okay," I replied, surprised at how easy it sounded.

🚶
We started walking side by side. Like it was the most natural thing.

"Ah, sorry," she added suddenly. "I didn't introduce myself. My name's Noa."

"Sora," I said. "Nice to meet you."

"Noa," she repeated softly, then paused. "…Sora?"

The way she said my name made me glance at her. She looked thoughtful, like she'd just remembered something but couldn't quite place it.

"Is something wrong?" I asked.

"No," she said quickly, shaking her head. "Just… thought I'd heard that name before."

Before I could respond, the sound of students moving toward the classroom pulled my attention back to the present.

"We should hurry," I said. "Class is about to start."

"Yeah," Noa agreed, smiling again. "Let's go."

We stepped inside together.

🌸 A Small Wonder

And for the first time since arriving at this university, I wondered—
Did I just make a friend?

🌸 Sora

Some meetings feel accidental.
Most of them are.

But every now and then,
you wonder if the universe nudged you just a little.

His Regret
The past you buried has a way of walking back in.
🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 · Chapter 2
POV: Ren Regret Memory

He seems like a nice guy.

That was the first thought that crossed my mind as I looked at Haruki beneath the sakura tree. Quiet. A little distant. The kind of person who listens more than he talks.

"So," I said, standing up, dusting off my hands, "I should probably get going."

Haruki glanced up.

"Oh. Okay. I was thinking of sitting here a bit longer, if you don't mind."

"Yeah," I replied with a small smile. "See you around."

"See you."

I walked away, leaving him under the tree, petals drifting lazily around him.

The university hallways were crowded. Students moved past me in groups—laughing, complaining, planning their next class. Everyone looked like they belonged somewhere.

🏫
I was scared to talk to him at first. But I'm glad I did.

After what happened with Sora… I hadn't really made friends. I kept my distance. Built walls. Told myself it was safer that way.

The memory crept in anyway.

Rain. Streetlights. My voice shaking as I said the words out loud.

💭 Memory

"I really like you."

I swallowed.

After that day, everything went wrong. Rumors spread. Whispers followed. And then she was gone.

Transferred.

It's my fault, I told myself, like I always did. I hurt her. I pushed her away. I shouldn't have confessed.

Regret sat heavy in my chest, familiar and unwelcome.

I lowered my gaze, walking faster—

—and then I felt it.

That strange pull. The kind that makes you look up without knowing why.

Our eyes met.

Just for half a second.

She was walking with a friend, her face calm, unreadable. Like nothing had happened. Like she hadn't just cracked something open inside me.

She looked away first and kept walking.

My steps slowed to a stop.

🌧️ Half a Second

She looked away first.
And I stood there, unable to.

"…Sora."

The name slipped out before I could stop it.

I turned, watching her disappear into the crowd. My heart pounded, loud in my ears.

Why is she here?

Or… was that really her?

The hallway swallowed her whole.

And I stood there, surrounded by people, realizing that the past I thought I'd buried had just walked straight back into my life.

💭 Ren

You tell yourself you've moved on.
You almost believe it.

And then a half-second glance
undoes everything.
