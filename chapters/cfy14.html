🔍 Case File: You · Chapter 14

Final Verdict — Part 2

Eight chairs, one empty. In the dark, The 8 turn on each other — and across the city, a trap closes on Oak Street.

Mystery Investigation Dark By AKA
The Grand Sin — Eight Chairs

Unknown — POV

The room was dark.

Not because the lights were off.

There simply weren't any.

Eight chairs stood in a circle, each separated by a few feet of empty floor.

Seven were occupied.

One was empty.

A single candle burned in the center.

Its flame moved even though there was no wind.

Lust sat with one leg crossed over the other, staring at the empty chair.

"Where is Pride?"

No one answered.

Lust sighed.

"Of course."

Across the room, Envy remained silent.

Their eyes were fixed on the candle.

Then a voice came from the darkness.

"Begin."

Everyone became still.

The candle went out.

For a moment, there was nothing.

Then—

A projector flickered on.

A photograph appeared on the wall.

🎞 On the Wall
01 Ruby Zeni.
02 Charlotte.
03 Casper.
04 Eli Forst.
05 Mia.

One after another.

The victims.

The room remained silent.

Lust smiled.

"Beautiful."

Envy looked at them.

"Beautiful?"

"The pattern."

Lust leaned forward.

"Everything leaves something behind."

Revenge spoke from the darkness.

"Except the dead."

Lust laughed quietly.

"That is why we keep making them speak."

Envy finally looked away from the screen.

"Enough."

Lust turned toward them.

"What?"

"You're enjoying this too much."

Lust smiled.

"That's what I was made for."

Silence.

Then a distorted voice came from the speaker.

"Sengoku-sama has given his order."

Every member of The 8 lowered their heads.

Except Envy.

Lust noticed.

"You didn't bow."

Envy slowly looked at them.

"I don't bow to voices."

The room changed.

Not physically.

But everyone felt it.

Lust's smile disappeared.

"Careful."

Envy stood.

"I have been careful."

They looked at the photographs.

"At first, I thought we were fulfilling desires."

Their voice was calm.

"But lately…"

They looked directly at Lust.

"I think we're fulfilling someone else's."

Lust stood as well.

"You're questioning Sengoku-sama?"

"No."

Envy paused.

"I'm questioning you."

A chair scraped against the floor.

Lust stepped closer.

"You should choose your next words carefully."

"I already did."

Envy reached into their coat.

Lust's hand moved instinctively.

But Envy didn't pull out a weapon.

They pulled out a photograph.

And dropped it on the floor.

Lust looked down.

Their expression changed.

It was a photograph of Rei.

Standing beside someone whose face had been cut out.

📷 Photograph

On the back was a date.
And beneath it—
15.08.2023
Sengoku's execution date.

Lust stared at it.

"Where did you get this?"

Envy didn't answer.

Lust picked it up.

"You've been hiding this?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because I wanted to know whether you knew."

Lust's eyes narrowed.

"Knew what?"

Envy stepped closer.

"That Rei wasn't working for Sengoku-sama."

A pause.

"He was working for whoever is pretending to be him."

Nobody moved.

Then Lust laughed.

A quiet laugh.

Almost amused.

"You're lying."

"Am I?"

"You expect me to believe Sengoku-sama isn't giving the orders?"

"No."

Envy looked toward the empty chair.

"I expect you to ask yourself why his chair is empty."

Lust stared at it.

For the first time—

they looked afraid.

Sato — 7:03 PM

Sato stared at the photograph.

Charlotte.

Ruby.

Two people connected by blood.

And both were dead.

He placed the photograph beside Ruby's case file.

Then Charlotte's.

Then Rei's.

Something was wrong.

Not with the murders.

With the order.

Ruby had been killed first.

Then Casper.

Then Mia.

Charlotte.

Each murder looked separate.

Different victims.

Different circumstances.

But there was one thing connecting all of them.

Someone wanted Sato to find them.

The symbols.

The letter.

The DNA.

The apartment.

The clues weren't mistakes.

They were invitations.

Sato leaned back.

"Why me?"

Aizwa stood near the window.

"You think they're communicating with you?"

Sato didn't answer.

He remembered the black figure outside the shop.

The photograph in Rei's apartment.

The symbol.

The voice on the phone.

Se-sama.

Sengoku-sama.

A dead man giving orders.

Sato closed his eyes.

Then something occurred to him.

"Akane."

Aizwa looked at him.

"What about her?"

Sato opened his eyes.

"Nothing."

He stood.

"I'm going back to the agency."

Akane — 8:12 PM

Akane was alone.

The agency was almost empty.

She sat at her desk, staring at the same file for nearly ten minutes.

She wasn't reading it anymore.

She was thinking about Sato.

About how things had changed.

Before Aizwa arrived, Sato would ask for her opinion.

Now—

"Aizwa."

"Aizwa, come with me."

"Aizwa, check this."

Aizwa.

Always Aizwa.

Akane shut the file.

"Whatever."

She stood.

As she walked past Sato's desk, something caught her attention.

A piece of paper.

She stopped.

It was covered in numbers.

She picked it up.

At the bottom was a single word.

🔢 Note

DESIRE.

Akane frowned.

Then—

her phone rang.

Unknown number.

She answered.

"Hello?"

Nothing.

Only breathing.

"Who's this?"

Still nothing.

Then a voice whispered:

"Detective Arima."

Akane froze.

"How do you know my name?"

A pause.

Then—

"You should stop looking."

The call ended.

Akane stared at the phone.

Her first instinct was to call Sato.

She didn't.

Instead, she looked toward his empty desk.

And for the first time—

she wondered whether Sato had been hiding something from her too.

Lust — 11:46 PM

Lust walked alone through an empty street.

The address Envy had given them was correct.

Oak Street.

Apartment 307.

Lust stopped outside the building.

They checked the photograph.

Same address.

Same building.

They smiled.

"So this is the toy."

They walked inside.

The hallway was completely silent.

Apartment 307 was at the end.

Lust knocked.

Once.

Twice.

Nothing.

They opened the door.

Unlocked.

Lust stepped inside.

The room was dark.

A chair sat in the middle.

A single photograph rested on it.

Lust picked it up.

Their smile vanished.

The photograph showed Envy.

Standing outside this exact building.

Taken only a few hours earlier.

📷 Photograph

On the back were four words.
YOU WERE NEVER THE TARGET.

The door slammed shut behind them.

Lust turned.

Someone was standing at the end of the hallway.

A black figure.

The same figure Sato had seen.

Lust took one step backward.

"Who are you?"

The figure didn't answer.

Instead, a voice came from the darkness.

"Envy sends their regards."

Lust's expression hardened.

"...Envy."

The figure disappeared.

Lust ran toward the door.

Locked.

They pulled harder.

Nothing.

Then they heard something.

A phone ringing.

Their own phone.

Lust slowly answered.

"Hello?"

Envy's voice.

Calm.

Almost emotionless.

"You wanted a toy."

Lust said nothing.

"I gave you one."

"You set me up."

"Yes."

The honesty caught Lust off guard.

"Why?"

A pause.

Then Envy whispered:

"Because I'm tired of being someone's desire."

The call ended.

Lust looked at the photograph again.

On the back, beneath the four words, another sentence had appeared.

📷 Beneath the Photograph

THE GRAND SIN HAS ALREADY BEGUN.

Sato — Rooftop

Sato stood on the agency rooftop.

The city was almost asleep.

He looked up at the sky.

For some reason, he thought about the first murder.

The vine shop.

The symbol.

The bleeding eye.

Then Ruby.

Mia.

Casper.

Charlotte.

He whispered:

"Four desires."

The Desires
POWER
GREED
CONTROL
REVENGE

He stopped.

"Lust."

His eyes widened.

The murders weren't random.

They were chapters.

Someone was writing them.

And everyone involved—

was a character.

Sato reached into his pocket.

The folded letter from Charlotte was still there.

He opened it again.

This time, he read the final line.

His face changed.

The sentence was simple.

But it wasn't addressed to Ruby.

It was addressed to someone else.

✉ Letter — Final Line

"If Sengoku-sama ever comes back, tell him I never stopped waiting."

Sato slowly lowered the paper.

Behind him—

the rooftop door opened.

Akane stepped outside.

"Sato."

He turned.

She looked serious.

"I got a call."

"From who?"

"I don't know."

She handed him her phone.

"There was only one thing they said."

Sato looked at her.

"What?"

Akane swallowed.

"They told me to stop looking."

Sato stared at the city.

Then he smiled.

Not because he was happy.

Because he finally understood.

"They're scared."

Akane frowned.

"Of what?"

Sato folded Charlotte's letter.

"Of us finding the truth."

A voice came from behind them.

"You're wrong."

Both detectives turned.

Aizwa stood in the doorway.

His expression was unreadable.

"They aren't scared of you."

Sato stared at him.

Aizwa continued.

"They're waiting for you."

Silence.

And somewhere far away—

a candle was lit.

Eight chairs.

Seven people.

One empty seat.

And a voice whispered:

End of Chapter 14
"Let the Grand Sin begin."
