Chapter Two

Feelings

✿
Rika — POV

I should try to set them up.
Honestly, when I think about it…
They would make such an amazing couple.
Hehe.

But how should I do it?
First, I need to talk to Kazuki.
But…
I don't even know how to start a conversation with him.
He's so quiet.

Hmm.
Maybe I should just approach him directly.
Or maybe I should observe him first.
Yeah…
This might be harder than I thought.
But still…
Helping Sayori is worth it.

✿   ·   ✿
Kazuki's Home
Kazuki — POV

I was lying on my bed, feeling bored.
Just another normal evening.
But somehow…
Sayori is on my mind again.

I don't know why.
But she keeps appearing in my thoughts.
Her voice.
Her smile.
The way she looks when she zones out in class.

Why am I thinking about her so much?
What do people call this feeling?

I stare at the ceiling.
Then a thought suddenly appears.
Maybe…
It's love.

But is it really love?
Or do I just like her personality?
What exactly do I like about her?
Her honesty?
Her calmness?
Her real self?

I close my eyes.
What a mess.

· · ·
A few days passed. And nothing really happened.
✿   ·   ✿
Classroom — Before Lunch
Sayori — POV

What should I do?
I look at Rika across the desk.
"Rikaaa… say something."

Rika sighs.
"Well… I'm also confused."
She leans back in her chair.
"It's actually hard to start a conversation with him."
She looks at me.
"How did you even do it, Sayori?"

I blink.
"Me?"
"I just said hi first."

Rika rubs her chin thoughtfully.
"Hmm…"

I hesitate for a moment.
Then another thought comes to my mind.
"Rika… I have a doubt."
"What?"
"Does he really see me as his friend?"

Rika stares at me.
Then she laughs.
"Wow."
"Amazing doubt… after all this time."

I pout slightly.
"Hey!"
Rika points at me.
"If you want the answer, confirm it yourself."

I sigh.
"Meanie."

✿   ·   ✿
Pink Sakura Tree — Lunch Time
Sayori — POV

I arrived here first today.
But Kazuki isn't here yet.
That's unusual.
I look around.
What's taking him so long?
Where is he?

A few minutes later, I see him walking toward the bench.

Kazuki — POV

Oh.
Sayori is already here.
That's not surprising.
After all, I told her she could come here anytime.
I walk closer.
"Hi, Sayori."
"What's going on?"
She looks slightly nervous.
"Eh… yo… hi."
"Nothing much."

Then suddenly…
She asks something directly.
"Hey, Kazuki…"
"What am I to you?"

I freeze for a moment.
What…?
That question caught me off guard.
What is she to me?

I think for a second.
Then the obvious answer comes out.
"You?"
"You're my friend."
I tilt my head slightly.
"Didn't you say we were friends?"

Sayori smiles.
But something about it feels a little forced.
"Oh… right."
"Yeah… hehe."

Sayori — POV

Well…
I was expecting a different answer.
But…
At least he thinks of me as a friend.
For now…
That's enough.
