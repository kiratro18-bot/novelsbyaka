🔍 Case File: You · Chapter 13

Final Verdict

In a silent apartment, a letter changes everything. And elsewhere, a new target is chosen.

Mystery Investigation Dark By AKA
Oak Street — Apartment 105

Sato — POV

Sato stepped out of the car and looked toward the apartment building.

"We're late, Aizwa."

Aizwa glanced toward the entrance.

"Looks like they finished their job faster than we expected."

Sato's expression hardened.

"Call forensics. And have the police secure the entire area."

"Understood."

Aizwa immediately reached for her radio.

Sato entered the apartment.

The room was silent.

Too silent.

His eyes moved across the scene until they stopped on the body lying on the floor.

He approached slowly.

"Charlotte…"

It was the same woman from the photograph they had found at Rei's apartment.

Sato examined the scene from a distance.

🔎 Scene Examination
01 Female.
02 Middle-aged.
03 Multiple injuries.
04 Evidence at the scene indicated that she had been assaulted before her death.
05 A knife wound had been inflicted afterward.

Sato stood back up.

Then he noticed something.

Something in her hand.

"Aizwa…"

He crouched beside the body.

"There's something here."

He carefully removed it.

A folded piece of paper.

Sato opened it.

His eyes moved across the first few lines.

Then stopped.

✉ Letter

Dear Ruby Zeni,
My dear daughter,
How have you been? I haven't heard from you in a while...

Sato stopped reading.

His expression changed.

"Ruby Zeni…"

He looked at the body again.

The name echoed in his mind.

Ruby was one of our victims.

He folded the letter back up.

"Aizwa."

She looked over.

"Yeah?"

"Handle the scene. I'll be back soon."

Aizwa frowned.

"Where are you going?"

But Sato had already turned away.

Sato

Sato — POV

Ruby Zeni.

He walked through the hallway, thoughts racing.

Ruby Zeni
01 She was murdered in her bathroom.
02 Before her death, she had met Rei for an adult film shoot.
03 Rei's DNA was found at the scene.
04 But Rei had an alibi.

So why was Charlotte carrying a letter addressed to Ruby?

Sato stopped walking.

What am I missing?

He looked down at the evidence bag containing the letter.

Ruby and Charlotte.

Mother and daughter.

Both connected to the case.

His eyes narrowed.

"There's a connection…"

But someone had gone to great lengths to hide it.

And now—

Someone had gone to even greater lengths to make sure Sato found it.

Unknown

Unknown — POV

Lust sat alone in a dark room.

"I need another toy…"

Their fingers tapped impatiently against the table.

"I want something new."

A voice came from behind.

"Looking for something?"

Lust turned.

"Envy."

Envy smiled.

"I have someone for you."

They placed a piece of paper on the table.

An address was written across it.

Lust picked it up.

"What?"

They looked at Envy.

"For real?"

Envy laughed.

"Of course."

Lust smiled.

"Thanks."

Envy's expression suddenly became serious.

"But not today."

Lust frowned.

"Why?"

"First…"

Envy pointed toward the door.

"…you attend the Grand Sin."

Lust sighed.

"Yeah, yeah. I'll be there."

The paper remained in Lust's hand.

The address waited.

And somewhere in the city—

Another desire had already chosen its next target.
