The cursor blinked.

That was the only thing moving in the dark office.

Kaito rubbed his tired eyes and glanced at the clock. 02:47 AM. Another night shift. Another pile of dead people's memories to clean.

He worked for Archive Corp, the company responsible for storing human consciousness after death. Every thought, memory, and emotion was preserved in a digital archive. Families could visit loved ones whenever they wanted. At least that was what the advertisements claimed.

Kaito's job was less glamorous. He deleted corrupted files. Memories that glitched. Archives that broke. Ghosts made of damaged data.

FILE OPENED
Subject: Unknown
Status: Deceased
Date of Death: 10 Years Ago
Last Login: 5 Minutes Ago ⚡

Kaito froze. That wasn't possible. Dead archives couldn't log in. He checked the data again. Same result. Five minutes ago. A chill crawled up his spine.

He opened the file. The screen flashed white. Then a room appeared. A small bedroom. A girl sat on the floor. She looked around seventeen. Long black hair. Gray hoodie. Fear in her eyes.

The moment she saw Kaito, she stood up.

Unknown Subject "Finally."

Kaito nearly fell out of his chair. Archives never reacted to visitors. They repeated stored memories. Nothing more. Yet this girl stared directly at him.

Unknown Subject "You can hear me, right?"
Kaito "...What are you?"

The girl's expression tightened.

Unknown Subject "You don't have much time."

Every monitor in the office flickered. The room's lights dimmed. The archive system emitted a warning tone.

Kaito "Who are you?"
Unknown Subject "My name is Aira."

She took a shaky breath.

"And if you're seeing this, then they've finally found me."
Kaito "Who?"
Aira "The people deleting history."

Kaito laughed nervously. "Okay, that's not funny."

Aira "I'm serious."

She stepped closer.

"I'm not a memory."

The words hit him like a truck.

Aira "I'm alive."

Kaito stared. Impossible. The file clearly listed her as deceased.

Aira "Ten years ago, I discovered something hidden inside the archive network."

The lights flickered again. This time harder.

Aira "The world isn't what you think it is."

Every instinct screamed at Kaito to close the file. Yet curiosity held him hostage.

Kaito "What did you find?"
Aira "The world is a simulation."
SYSTEM INTERRUPT

The office suddenly lost power. Darkness swallowed everything. Every monitor turned black. Then one screen reignited. A single message appeared.

UNAUTHORIZED ACCESS DETECTED

Kaito's heartbeat accelerated. Aira's image distorted. Pixels broke apart.

Aira "No... They found us."

The archive tried to shut itself down. Kaito slammed commands into his keyboard. The system resisted. Files disappeared. Records vanished. Entire archives were being erased in real time. Millions of lives. Gone.

Kaito "What is happening?"
Aira "Every person who discovers the truth gets deleted."
Kaito "Deleted?"
Aira "Not killed."

She shook her head.

"Worse."

She pointed toward the endless sea of digital files.

"They erase every record that you ever existed."

Kaito opened a search window. He typed random names. Thousands returned errors. Empty spaces. Missing data. People who had vanished completely. As if they had never lived. His stomach twisted. Aira was right. Someone was cleaning history. And they were doing it on a massive scale.

Suddenly the walls of the archive began cracking. The virtual room collapsed around her. Chunks of reality disappeared. Aira backed away.

Aira "Kaito. If I disappear, find the coordinates."
Kaito "What coordinates?"

She quickly typed numbers onto the screen.

PURGE INITIATED
37.2691° N   /   114.0579° W
PROMISE ME
Aira "They're coming."

She smiled sadly.

"For once..."

Her voice broke.

"...look beyond the sky."

Then she vanished. The archive closed. Silence returned. Kaito sat frozen. The coordinates remained. Nothing else.

72 HOURS LATER

For three days he searched. No sleep. No rest. No answers. Eventually the coordinates led him to an abandoned research facility buried beneath a desert. It looked centuries old. Rust covered every wall. Dust filled the air.

At the center of the facility stood a single metal door. Locked. Beside it was a scanner. Kaito entered the coordinates. The door opened.

Inside was a cryogenic chamber. One pod remained active. A girl slept inside. Long black hair. Gray hoodie.

Aira.

Alive.

His breath caught. The pod hissed open. Aira slowly opened her eyes. For several seconds neither spoke. Then she smiled.

Aira "You actually came."
Kaito "I still don't know if you're insane."
Aira "Maybe."

She climbed out.

"But we're running out of time."

The facility shook violently. Alarms echoed everywhere. Red lights flooded the room. Aira grabbed his hand. "Follow me." They ran through collapsing hallways. Machines exploded. Steel doors ripped apart. Finally they reached a massive elevator. The doors opened. Aira stepped inside. Kaito followed.

ASCENDING

The elevator rose. Higher. Higher. Higher. Until suddenly — the ceiling above them disappeared. The world shattered. The sky cracked like glass. Light exploded in every direction. Reality itself broke apart.

Kaito shielded his eyes. When the light faded — he froze.

There was no Earth. No cities. No oceans. No sky. Only darkness. Stars stretched endlessly beyond giant windows. They stood aboard a colossal spacecraft drifting through space. Ancient. Silent. Dying.

Aira "The simulation was created after Earth died."
Kaito "Died?"
Aira "Centuries ago. Humanity couldn't survive."

She looked toward countless cryogenic pods filling the ship. Millions of sleeping people. Entire civilizations dreaming.

"The simulation became humanity's last home."

Kaito stared at the endless stars. All his life. Everything he knew. Every memory. Every city. Every person. A dream. A beautiful lie. Yet for the first time, the stars above him were real. No code. No simulation. No illusion. Just reality.

Aira stood beside him. Together they watched the universe. And for the first time in his life — Kaito looked at a real sky.
