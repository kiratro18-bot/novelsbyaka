Forgiveness?

The kind of words that take years to say.

POV: Sora & Ren Confession Honesty By AKA

POV: Sora

Why was he sitting at the next table the whole time?

Why did he still remember me… after what I did?

I couldn't focus on the food. My hands felt cold, even though the cafeteria was warm. Every second stretched too long.

Noa's phone buzzed.

"Oh—sorry," she said, standing up. "I need to take this call. I'll be right back."

"That's fine," I replied, forcing a smile.

And then she was gone.

Just like that, it was only us.

💭 Sora

The silence between Ren and me
felt heavier than any argument ever could.

POV: Ren Quiet Truth

POV: Ren

She was sitting right in front of me.

Not a memory. Not a regret.
Sora.

I stared at my hands, trying to figure out how to start without breaking something.

Finally, I spoke.

"So… Sora," I said quietly. "How are you?"

A simple question. Too simple for everything it carried.

"I—I'm doing fine," she answered. "What about you?"

"I'm okay," I said. "I'm… glad to see you're doing well."

It was true.

POV: Sora Confession Tears

POV: Sora

My chest tightened.

Don't cry.
Not here. Not now.

I had imagined this moment so many times—me explaining everything, him understanding, everything feeling lighter afterward.

But now that it was real… my words felt fragile.

"Ren," I said, my voice shaking. "I need to tell you something."

He looked up, attentive but cautious.

"Back then," I continued, "when you told me how you felt… I was happy."

His eyes flickered.

"My feelings were the same," I admitted. "But I was scared."

I swallowed hard.

"I was going to transfer. I knew it. And I thought if we started something only to be separated… we'd both end up hurting more."

My hands clenched in my lap.

"So I ran. I didn't explain. I didn't give you a chance to understand."

My voice broke. "I chose the easy way out."

Tears blurred my vision.

"I know I don't have the right to ask," I whispered. "But… will you forgive me?"

POV: Ren Honesty Careful

POV: Ren

I looked at her for a long moment.

I saw the girl I once loved.
And the person she had become.

"I don't hate you," I said finally.

Her breath hitched.

"But forgiveness…" I continued slowly, "isn't something I can give just because you asked."

Her shoulders trembled.

"What you did hurt," I said honestly. "And it took me a long time to stop blaming myself for it."

I paused.

"But… I'm willing to talk. I'm willing to try."

I met her eyes.

"That's all I can promise right now."

The Fourth

A small decision. An enormous consequence.

POV: Haruki Collision Recognition By AKA

POV: Haruki

I checked the time.

There was still a little while left. I could make it to the cafeteria after all.

When I walked in, I spotted Noa near one of the tables, her phone pressed to her ear. She noticed me and raised a finger, signaling wait. I stopped a few steps away, pretending to scroll through my phone.

She hung up a moment later.

"Oh," she said, smiling. "You finally came."

"Yeah," I replied. "Work's done. Did you eat already?"

"No," she said, glancing toward another table. "I was about to, but my friend was talking to someone."

"Who?" I asked casually.

🍽️
University Cafeteria Where the past is always one table away.

"Sora," Noa called out, waving. "Oh—you're alone now. Where did that boy go?"

"He had some errand to run," Sora replied.

"Oh," Noa said. "Okay then. Come here—my friend's here now."

🌸
Haruki Turns
And the name hit harder than he expected.

I turned.

And froze.

Sora?

The name hit harder than I expected.

She was standing there, calm on the outside, but something in her eyes shifted the moment she saw me too.

No way…

Noa gestured between us. "This is my friend."

Sora took a small step forward. "Hello," she said politely. "Nice to meet you. My name is Sora."

For a second, I couldn't breathe.

"Myself… Haruki," I said.

Her eyes widened.

"Haruki?" she repeated, disbelief slipping into her voice.

I nodded once. "Yeah."

Noa blinked. "Wait—do you two know each other?"

I hesitated.

"She was in my school," I said simply.

The words felt heavier than they should have.

Sora didn't say anything.
