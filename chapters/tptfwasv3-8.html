University Festival: Day One

Things were… fine. At least on the surface.

POV: Noa, Sora, Haruki & Ren Backstage By AKA

POV: Noa

Things were… fine.

At least on the surface.

The four of us were assigned to the lighting team, running cables, fixing stands, adjusting switches backstage. Busy work. Loud work. Safe work.

But my eyes kept drifting.

Haruki was on the other side of the stage.

Ren was beside me, carrying lights.

And Sora was gone — sent off with Haruki to buy meals for everyone.

That bothered me more than I wanted to admit.

I wanted to enjoy the festival with him.

Not watch him walk away with someone else.

I shook my head and focused on the equipment.

🌸
Alone. With Haruki. Again.
Why does this keep happening?
🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 ·
POV: Sora Errand Run

POV: Sora

Alone.

With Haruki.

Again.

Why does this keep happening?

"Um… so," I said, trying to sound normal, "what exactly are we supposed to get?"

"Meals," Haruki replied. "For everyone."

"Oh. Right. I forgot."

Silence crept in.

My heart started racing for no reason.

And then—

I said the thing I shouldn't have.

"So… Haruki," I asked carefully, "are you in a relationship with Noa?"

He stopped walking.

"No," he said. Plain. Direct.

My breath caught.

"Oh…"

So he's single.

I didn't say it out loud, but the thought lingered longer than it should have.

🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 ·
POV: Haruki Distance

POV: Haruki

Why is she asking this?

I didn't look at her.

What does it matter now?

I didn't feel the same pull anymore.

Or maybe I was just tired of feeling anything at all.

Focus, I told myself.

Just get the food and go back.

University Hall · Backstage Our two leads are sick.

The drama team head rushed in, panic written all over his face.

"Guys, we've got a problem," he said. "Our two lead actors are sick. They ate something bad and can't perform."

Murmurs spread.

"We just need one boy and one girl," he added. "Anyone willing to step in?"

Silence.

Then—

"I'll do it."

Ren stepped forward.

"I'll take the male role."

Everyone turned.

Before anyone else could speak—

"I'll do it too."

Noa.

"I'll be the female lead."

Time froze.

Haruki's eyes widened.

Sora felt her chest tighten.

"No time to think," the team head said. "Costumes are ready. The act starts soon."

Ren and Noa were already being pulled away.

Haruki and Sora stood there, stunned.

The Promise Beneath the Tree

"If we ever get lost… let's meet here again."

POV: Sora, Haruki, Noa & Ren The Stage Play By AKA

The lights dimmed.

The chatter of the crowd softened into silence as the curtain rose.

On stage stood a single tree prop — bare branches stretching upward, simple and quiet.

Too quiet.

POV: Sora

My breath caught.

A tree…

Of course it was a tree.

Ren stood on stage, posture straight, eyes focused forward. Beside him was Noa, dressed plainly, her hands folded tightly in front of her.

They didn't look like actors.

They looked like people carrying something heavy.

🎭
Scene One — The Promise "If we ever get lost… let's meet here again."

Ren (as the boy):

"If we ever get lost… let's meet here again."

His voice was steady, but I heard it — the hesitation underneath.

Noa (as the girl):

"Even if time changes us?"

"Yes," he replied.

"Even then."

My fingers curled into my sleeves.

That's not just acting…

🌸
Why Am I Always Watching
Instead of stepping forward?
🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 ·
POV: Haruki Watching

POV: Haruki

I didn't mean to focus on Noa.

But she stood differently up there.

Confident. Clear. Like she chose to be seen.

When she spoke, the stage felt smaller — quieter — like the words were meant for someone specific.

And for a moment, I wondered:

Why am I always watching instead of stepping forward?

🎭
Scene Two — Separation "I was scared."

The girl on stage looked away.

Noa:

"I'm leaving."

Ren turned sharply.

Ren:

"When?"

"…Soon."

"Why didn't you tell me earlier?"

She hesitated.

"I was scared."

Those two words cracked something open.

This Is What I Did to Him
Apologies don't rewind time.
🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 ·
POV: Sora Regret

POV: Sora

My chest hurt.

I said those same words.

Scared.

Of distance.

Of pain.

Of choosing.

Ren's voice shook when he spoke again.

"So you'll just leave… without letting me decide?"

I pressed my lips together.

This is what I did to him.

I finally understood — not in my head, but in my body.

Apologies don't rewind time.

🎭
Scene Three — Reunion "We're different people now."

The lights shifted.

Years had passed.

The tree remained — dull, unblooming.

Ren stood beneath it again.

Noa approached slowly.

They faced each other, close but distant.

Noa:

"You waited."

Ren:

"I did."

Silence.

Then—

"We're different people now," she said softly.

Ren nodded.

"…Yeah."

They didn't touch.

They didn't cry.

They simply turned away from the tree — in opposite directions.

The lights went out.

POV: Noa

Applause thundered around us.

I glanced toward Haruki.

He was clapping.

But his eyes weren't smiling.

Did I cross a line?
