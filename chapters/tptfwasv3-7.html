University Festival

Four names. Four reactions.

POV: Narrator, Haruki, Sora, Noa & Ren Festival Prep By AKA

The classroom buzzed with half-awake chatter when the professor clapped his hands.

"For the upcoming university festival," he announced, "I need volunteers to assist the drama team."

A pause.

Then—

"Sora.
Noa.
Haruki.
Ren."

Four names.

Four reactions.

Silence fell between them like something fragile cracking.

"So," the professor continued, oblivious, "you'll be working together. Is that acceptable?"

Noa forced a smile.

"Uh… yeah. That's fine, right?"

"Y-yeah… sure," Sora said, a little too quickly.

Haruki didn't answer.

Ren smiled.

"Looking forward to it."

The professor nodded. "Good. Details later."

Just like that, it was decided.

Haruki's Answers

"So you're that boy she ran from."

POV: Haruki & Ren Confrontation Sakura Tree By AKA

POV: Haruki

What a mess.

The drama team was working like their lives depended on it — lights, props, voices overlapping. My head started pounding.

I need a break.

I slipped away without saying anything.

That's when I saw it.

The sakura tree.

Not blooming.

Bare branches. Dull. Quiet.

"Oh… right," I murmured. "The season's gone."

I sat beneath it anyway.

"Taking a break?"

I looked up.

"Oh. Hey, Ren."

"Yeah," I said. "What about you?"

"Same."

Silence settled between us, thick and uncomfortable. The kind that makes you hear your own thoughts too clearly.

Sakura Tree Bare branches. Quiet.

I Should Ask
Two boys, one question.
🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 ·
POV: Ren Resolve

POV: Ren

I stared at the tree.

Then at him.

I should ask.

If I don't now, I never will.

"Haruki," I said, forcing my voice steady, "you and Sora… are you close?"

That hit.

I saw it in his eyes.

"She was in my high school," he replied after a moment.

"Oh… I see."

I hesitated.

"I saw you two the other day. Talking under this tree."

"Oh. Yeah," he said. "We were."

Then he looked at me.

"What about you?"

My fingers curled slightly.

"We've been friends since childhood. Sora and me."

"Oh," he said softly.

Then—

"So you're that boy she ran from."

💭 Ren

My breath caught.
How does he know that?

"…Yes," I answered.

Silence.

No anger.

No accusation.

Just two boys sitting under a tree that had already lost its petals.
