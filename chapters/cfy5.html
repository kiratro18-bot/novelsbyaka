🔍 Case File: You · Chapter 5

Aftermath

DNA doesn't lie. But the dead don't rise either.

Mystery Investigation Dark By AKA

Akane — POV

The crime scene was brutal.

Even after years of experience, the image still lingered in Akane's mind.

Blood across the floor. Broken shelves. Signs of desperate resistance.

Whoever the killer was…
They were intelligent.
Too intelligent.

Right now, she sat beside Sato inside the agency office, waiting for the victim's full postmortem report to arrive.

The room was silent except for the sound of rain hitting the windows.

Five minutes later—

The office door opened.

A doctor stepped inside carrying several documents.

Sato immediately stood up.

"The report?"

The doctor handed him the file.

🗂 Postmortem Examination Report
01 27 stab wounds to the stomach area
02 Cause of death: excessive blood loss
03 Multiple bruises and defensive wounds
04 Sexual assault indicators present
05 Victim attempted to resist before being overpowered

The room fell silent.

Sato slowly lowered the file.

His expression darkened.

"What about the DNA test?" he asked quietly. "Did they analyze the semen sample?"

The doctor nodded.

"We did."

He handed over another sheet.

"Take a look."

Sato's eyes moved across the paper.

Then suddenly—

His expression froze.

🧬
DNA Analysis: Match Identified Semen Sample · Genetic Profile · Conclusive

Akane grabbed the report from his hand.

Her eyes widened.

"That's impossible."

Sato stared at her.

"Sengoku…?"

Akane nodded slowly.

"He was sentenced to death by hanging three years ago."

· · ·

Silence filled the room.

"So how can his DNA appear at a fresh crime scene?" she whispered.

Sato's mind raced.

Nothing about this made sense.
A dead criminal's DNA. No fingerprints. A killer who erased every trace.

Something was wrong.

Very wrong.

Suspect · Historical Case
Sengoku
Executed · 3 Years Ago
Status Death by hanging
Current DNA at crime scene
Possibility Impossible

Sato suddenly looked back at the doctor.

"The knife we recovered from the scene—what about fingerprint analysis?"

The doctor adjusted his glasses.

"There weren't any fingerprints on it."

Sato clenched his jaw.

"…Damn it."

The room became quiet again.

Then—

"Akane."

She looked at him.

"Do we still have Sengoku's old case files in the records archive?"

Akane nodded slowly.

"Yes. We should."

"Bring them to me."

Without another word, Akane left the room.

Sato remained standing alone beside the desk, staring at the DNA report in silence.

His thoughts spiraled deeper and deeper.

🔍 Questions Without Answers

Is this a setup?
Or something far more unexpected…?

A dead criminal's DNA.
Fresh blood.
No prints.
No traces.

How is this possible?
And why?

Outside the window, thunder echoed across the city.

Rain pounded against the glass.

And for the first time—
Sato began to feel that this case was no longer ordinary.

End of Chapter 5
