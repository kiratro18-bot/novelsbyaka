Chapters Three

The Turning Point

✿
Near the High School
Sayori — POV

Sayori was walking toward school like usual.
The morning air felt nice.
And strangely… She was feeling happy.

Kazuki had called her his friend.
Even though she was hoping for something more…
That word still made her smile.

She continued walking down the road, lost in her thoughts.
Thinking about him.
Thinking about yesterday.

Two minutes passed.

She didn't notice the sound of a car approaching.

Then suddenly—

BANG

A loud sound echoed through the street.
Her thoughts shattered.
Her vision blurred.
The world around her suddenly went white.

She couldn't understand what had just happened.

✿   ·   ✿
High School · Classroom
Kazuki — POV

Another school day.
Honestly, I wish it was Saturday.
School days feel so long sometimes.

I entered the classroom and sat down.

Then I noticed something again.

Sayori wasn't here.

That was surprising. She usually arrived earlier than most students.

I looked at the empty seat beside me.

Maybe she's just late again.

Class eventually started. The teacher walked into the room.

But something about his expression felt different.

He looked serious.

He stood in front of the class and cleared his throat.

Teacher: "Everyone… listen."

The classroom became quiet.

Teacher: "I… have something to share."

He paused for a moment.

Then he spoke again.

Teacher: "Sayori… is in the hospital."

My heart suddenly felt heavy.

The teacher continued.

Teacher: "She was hit by a car this morning."

For a moment…

The classroom felt completely silent.

End of Chapter Five

Chapter Three – Lost

Kazuki — POV

As soon as I heard the news…
I ran.

The hospital was far from the school, but I didn't care.
I just needed to get there.

Rika came with me.

Neither of us said much during the way there.
The silence between us felt heavy.

· · ·
Hospital
Sayori's Condition

Sayori had injuries.
Bandages around her arm.
A small cut near her forehead.

But the doctors said something else that shocked everyone even more.

She had woken up.

But something wasn't right.

She looked confused.
Lost.
Like she didn't know where she was supposed to be.

The doctor spoke to her parents quietly.

Doctor: "She remembers who she is."

Doctor: "But it seems she has lost many of her memories."

Sayori's parents looked worried.

Doctor: "There is a chance they may return…"

He paused.

Doctor: "But right now, we cannot be sure."

✿   ·   ✿
Hospital Bed
Sayori — POV

I slowly open my eyes.
Everything around me feels strange.
White walls. Machines. A quiet room.

I don't remember how I got here.

Was I always here…?

Something feels missing.

Like pieces of my life disappeared.

I turn my head toward the window.

Outside the room, I see a girl around my age.
She is sitting near the hallway window.
Writing something in a small journal.
There is a red ribbon tied around her wrist.

Something about her feels… different.

Then she looks up at me.

Our eyes meet.

She speaks first.

Girl: "Lost your memories?"

I blink in confusion.

Sayori: "What…?"

Girl: "Try remembering the person you love."

She closes her journal.

Girl: "Or the person who loves you."

Girl: "Sometimes… that helps memories return."

I didn't fully understand what she meant.

And before I could ask her name…

She stood up and walked away.

✿   ·   ✿
Outside the Hospital
Kazuki — POV

I stop in front of the hospital entrance.

My chest still feels tight.

Kazuki: "This is the place… right, Rika?"

Rika nods slowly.

Rika: "Yeah… Kazuki."

I look at the building.

Somewhere inside… Sayori is there.

But now…

I don't know if she remembers me.
