The Past

Was this some kind of punishment?

POV: Sora & Noa Confrontation Truth By AKA

POV: Sora

Things really went to hell fast.

First Ren.

Now Haruki.

Was this some kind of punishment?

Or was this just the price of running away?

I exhaled slowly and looked at Haruki, who stood across from me like a stranger wearing a familiar face.

"So…" I said, forcing my voice steady. "How are you these days, Haruki?"

"I'm doing fine," he replied. His tone was polite. Too polite.

"What about you?"

"Me too."

And then—

Silence.

🌸 Not the Comfortable Kind

The kind that presses against your ears
until you want to scream just to break it.

Noa shifted beside me, clearly sensing it. I swallowed and decided to step forward before this moment swallowed me instead.

"Haruki," I said softly, "about that day when you—"

"I don't want to hear my past."

His words cut clean.

"Not for now, Sora."

He didn't look at me again. Just turned to Noa.

"I've got class," he said. "I'll see you later."

And just like that, he walked away.

I stayed frozen, staring at the empty space he left behind.

POV: Noa

That was… strange.

I watched Haruki disappear into the crowd, something heavy settling in my chest.

"He acted really weird," I said quietly.

"Sora… what past were you talking about?"

She didn't answer immediately.

"So," she said instead, "he didn't tell you?"

I shook my head. "No."

She looked down, fingers tightening around the edge of the table.

Should I tell her?

I could see the question written all over her face.

"If you want to," I added gently. "You can tell me."

Confront

It felt like I dropped a bomb between us.

POV: Noa & Sora Truth Resolve By AKA

POV: Noa

It was still early when I reached Haruki's place.

I knocked once, then waited—heart beating faster than it should've.

The door opened.

"Oh," Haruki said, surprised. "You're here. This is rare."

I crossed my arms. "What, I can't pick you up once in a while?"

He gave a small smile. "Didn't say that. Let's go."

As we walked toward the university together, I kept glancing at him from the corner of my eye.

I want to be with him.

And I want the truth.

The silence stretched.

So I broke it.

"Um… Haruki," I said carefully.

"That letter in high school… was it for us?"

It felt like I dropped a bomb between us.

He stopped walking.

"…Yes."

Then, almost immediately, he sighed. "I get it now. Sora must've told you."

"She did," I replied. "The first letter—your name was written for Sora."

"I know," he said quietly.

"Then why didn't you tell me?" I asked. "Why hide it?"

He looked away, jaw tightening.

"Because I didn't want the past to repeat itself," he said.

"I was scared, Noa. Scared of getting hurt the same way again."

He paused.

"I'm sorry."

I swallowed.

"It's okay," I said. And I meant it—at least on the surface.

But inside, something didn't sit right.

He dodged it.

Not the truth… but the feeling.

💭 Noa

It was like he didn't care anymore.
Or maybe—he cared too much once, and that changed him.

🏫
University Entrance Where the truth couldn't quite catch up.

"We're finally here," I said, forcing cheer into my voice.

Then I saw her.

"Oh—hey!" I waved. "Sora!"

She turned, surprised. "Hey, Noa… hey, Haruki."

"Hii…" Haruki replied, avoiding her eyes.

"I should get going," he added quickly. "Bye. See you soon."

And then—he left.

Just like that.

POV: Sora

He didn't even look at me.

Not properly.

"He's ignoring me," I said softly once he was gone.

My chest felt tight, like I was holding my breath without realizing it.

"Noa," I said, turning to her, "will you help me?"

She looked at me, listening.

"I need to say sorry to him," I continued.

"Even if he doesn't accept it. Even if he hates me."

I clenched my fists.

"I just… need to say it."

Noa nodded. "Sure."

And for the first time that day, I felt like maybe—just maybe—I wasn't completely alone.
