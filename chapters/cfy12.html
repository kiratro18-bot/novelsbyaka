Revenge

Behind a locked door, revenge finally speaks. And elsewhere, someone is quietly left behind.

Mystery Investigation Dark By AKA
Unknown

Unknown — POV

Two figures stood outside an apartment building.

Revenge looked toward the door.

"We're here."

Lust glanced at the apartment number.

"Who is this woman?"

Revenge's expression hardened.

"Someone I hate."

Lust looked at them curiously.

"And?"

"And I'll do anything to get my revenge."

Lust sighed.

"Can't you at least tell me what she did?"

Revenge ignored the question.

"Let's finish what we came here for."

Lust followed.

"Fine."

Apartment 105

Bang. Bang. Bang.

Someone was pounding on the door.

A woman inside hurried toward it.

"Yeah, yeah! I'm coming!"

She opened the door.

Two figures stood in front of her.

The woman's expression immediately changed.

"Who—who are you?"

Revenge stepped forward.

"Lust."

Lust nodded.

Revenge grabbed the woman and forced her away from the doorway.

Moments later, she was restrained in a chair.

The woman struggled.

"What do you want from me?!"

Revenge looked at Lust.

"Do your work."

Lust hesitated for a moment.

Then nodded.

"Yeah…"

The room fell silent.

Some time later.

Lust stepped away.

"I'm done."

Revenge looked toward the woman.

"Then it's my turn."

The door remained closed.

No one outside heard what happened next.

Detective Agency

Sato was standing beside the evidence board.

"What did you find about the woman?"

Aizwa opened her laptop.

"I ran a background check."

She turned the screen toward him.

"Her name is Charlotte. She's half-American and was previously married."

Akane looked over.

"What about her husband?"

Aizwa scrolled through the records.

"Dead."

Sato's eyes narrowed.

"Name?"

"Hiroshi."

Sato looked toward the photograph on the evidence board.

"Where did Charlotte live?"

Aizwa checked the address.

"Oak Street. Apartment 105."

Sato immediately grabbed his coat.

"Let's go, Aizwa."

Aizwa nodded.

"Yep."

They headed toward the exit.
