🕰 Case File: You · Chapter 6

The Past

Some cases close. Some only pretend to.

Mystery Flashback Dark By AKA
Three Years Ago

Chief — POV

"Sengoku…"

Even now, the name left a bitter taste in his mouth.

Back then, Sengoku was one of the most notorious criminals in the city.

A man who seemed to enjoy killing.
A man responsible for countless crimes.
And somehow…
A man who always managed to escape.

Every time the police got close, he vanished.
Every time they thought they had him cornered, he slipped away.

But then one day—

He didn't run.

The chief still remembered it clearly.

The crime scene.
The blood.
The flashing police lights.

And Sengoku standing in the middle of it all.
Waiting.
As if he wanted to be arrested.

The chief had personally placed the handcuffs on him.

Soon after, Sengoku was sentenced to death.

Case closed.

Or at least…
That's what everyone believed.

The chief lowered his head.

I should have looked deeper into it back then.

Present Day

Sato — POV

Sato frowned.

"What do you mean, you should've looked deeper?"

The chief remained silent for a moment before answering.

"Sengoku wasn't acting alone."

Sato's eyes narrowed.

"What are you talking about?"

The chief leaned back in his chair.

"He ran an organization known as The 8."

"The 8?"

Sato searched his memory.

Nothing.

"I've never heard of them."

The chief nodded.

"Most people haven't."

"What happened to them?"

"No one knows."

The chief stared at the ceiling.

"After Sengoku was sentenced, the group disappeared."

"Disappeared?"

"Or at least… that's what the records say."

The room grew quiet.

Sato thought for a moment.

"What about the members?"

The chief shook his head.

"No names. No identities. Nothing useful."

Then suddenly—

The chief looked directly at Sato.

"You found it, didn't you?"

Sato blinked.

"Found what?"

"The apartment key."

A brief silence followed.

Sato's expression changed.

"…You knew about it?"

The chief nodded.

"Yeah."

Sato's hand instinctively moved toward his pocket.

The key was still there.

"You kept it in the records?"

"I did."

"Why?"

The chief looked away.

"Because I wanted someone else to finish what I couldn't."

Sato stared at him.

"You want me to go there?"

"Yes."

"Alone?"

The chief's answer came immediately.

"Alone."

Three Years Ago · Prison Visit

Sengoku sat behind the glass wall with a relaxed smile.

As if he wasn't awaiting execution.
As if nothing mattered.

The chief sat across from him.

For several moments, neither spoke.

Then Sengoku laughed.

"You know…"

The chief remained silent.

"I'm probably the worst criminal you've ever met, right?"

"I don't know."

"Come on."

Sengoku grinned.

"You know I am."

Then suddenly, he reached into his pocket.

The guards immediately tensed.

But instead of a weapon—

He pulled out a small key.

The chief frowned.

"A key?"

Sengoku slid it across the table.

"My apartment."

The chief stared at it.

"Why are you giving this to me?"

Sengoku shrugged.

"Don't know."

The smile on his face widened.

"Maybe you'll find something interesting."

🔍 What Was Inside

Photographs.
Crime scene pictures.
Alcohol bottles scattered everywhere.

Suspicious white powder stored in containers.

And one final item.
A metal badge.

Present Day

The chief stared silently at the office window.

Back then, he had accepted the key.
And he had gone to the apartment.

What he found inside still bothered him.

The chief slowly closed his eyes.

The symbol was engraved onto it.

Three lines.
A triangle.
A bleeding eye.

And beneath it—

Only three words.
