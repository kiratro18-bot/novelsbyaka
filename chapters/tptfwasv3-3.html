A Decision to Make

The kind of fear that looks like moving on.

POV: Sora Fear Relief By AKA

Our eyes met.

For a moment, my mind refused to accept what my body already knew. The hallway noise faded, and all I could hear was my own breath.

It couldn't be him. Not here. Not like this.

I didn't stop. I didn't speak. I told myself it was just a stranger—someone who looked familiar, someone my memory was playing tricks on.

But doubt followed me with every step.

Was that him?

And if it was… What would I even say?

I was scared. Scared that one word would reopen everything I worked so hard to bury. Scared that staying silent would still hurt just as much.

So I walked away.

🌸 Sora

And somewhere inside that fear,
there was something else too—
something quieter, harder to admit.

Relief.

I hated myself for it.

I had no right to feel happy. Not after everything. But seeing him standing there, alive, moving forward…

…I was glad.

🌿 The Garden

The university garden was calmer than the hallways.
Sunlight filtered through leaves, soft and warm, almost gentle.

"Sora!"

I turned to see Noa waving at me, already carrying her lunch tray.

"Are you having lunch?" she asked.

"Yeah," I replied. "I was just about to."

"Then come with me," she said easily. "Let's eat together."

"I'd love to."

She hesitated for a second, then smiled again. "Ah—one thing. A friend of mine is joining too. A boy. Is that okay?"

"Oh," I said, surprised. "That's fine."

For some reason, my mind jumped ahead of itself. A boyfriend?

"No, no!" Noa said quickly, blushing as if she'd read my thoughts. "It's not like that."

"It's really okay," I assured her, smiling. "I don't mind."

She relaxed, laughing softly. "Good."

But even as we walked, a strange unease settled in my chest.

POV: Ren Recognition Courage

…Sora?

My heart skipped so hard it actually hurt. I turned my head slowly, afraid that if I moved too fast, the moment would disappear.

There she was.

Not a memory. Not a regret. Her.

So it really is her, I thought. The Sora I knew.

My chest tightened.

Should I talk to her?

What would I even say?

Silence stretched.

Cowardice threatened.

🚶
Then I stood up. Before I could stop myself.

I walked over before I could stop myself.

"Hey," I said, forcing my voice to stay calm. "How are you doing?"

Noa blinked, then leaned slightly toward Sora. "…Is he hitting on us?" she whispered.

Sora's breath caught.

"R-Ren…?" she murmured, barely audible. "Is that… you?"

Noa straightened, eyes widening. "Wait—you know him, Sora?"

Sora didn't answer. Her silence said more than words ever could.
