The Mark

A name surfaces. A photograph appears. And revenge finally shows its face.

Mystery Investigation Dark By AKA
Detective Agency

Aizwa walked into the office carrying a thin file.

"Agent Sato."

Sato looked up from his desk.

"Yeah?"

"I found the victim's real identity."

Sato immediately straightened.

"Who is he?"

Aizwa opened the file.

"His real name was Eli Forst. He was a businessman who disappeared from the public eye several years ago."

She turned another page.

"And there's something else."

Sato waited.

"The DNA recovered from the crime scene matches the sample we collected from Rei."

Sato's expression hardened.

"So Rei really was connected to him."

Aizwa nodded.

"Exactly."

Sato stood up.

"Then let's pick him up."

Akane grabbed her coat.

"Let's go, Sato."

Sato shook his head.

"You stay here."

Akane looked surprised.

"What?"

"Me and Aizwa will handle it."

Akane stared at him for a moment before shrugging.

"Sure."

Wow, she thought.

Sato turned toward Aizwa.

"Let's go, Agent."

Aizwa nodded.

"Yeah."

On the Way

The city passed silently outside the car window.

Sato stared ahead.

Something felt wrong.

Not just about Rei.

Not just about Eli Forst.

Something else.

A strange feeling had been growing inside him since the investigation began.

An urge.

A need to solve the case.

It wasn't normal.

Why do I feel like this?

Then he remembered the black figure from the remote crime scene.

That brief glimpse.

That feeling of being watched.

Who was that person?

"Agent Sato?"

Sato didn't respond.

"Sato?"

He blinked.

Aizwa was looking at him.

"You okay?"

Sato looked around.

"Yeah."

"You completely zoned out."

"No, no. I was just thinking."

Aizwa pointed toward the building ahead.

"We're here."

Sato looked through the windshield.

Rei's apartment.

"Let's go."

Rei's Apartment

Sato pressed the doorbell.

Nothing.

He rang it again.

Still nothing.

Aizwa looked at him.

"Think he's inside?"

Sato shook his head.

"Only one way to find out."

They forced the door open.

The apartment was empty.

"Rei?" Aizwa called.

No answer.

Sato slowly walked through the rooms.

Nothing.

No Rei.

No obvious signs of a struggle.

Then Aizwa stopped.

"Sato…"

He turned.

She was staring at the wall.

The symbol.

Again.

Three lines.

A triangle.

A bleeding eye.

But this time, there was something beside it.

A photograph.

A woman's face.

A thick red circle had been drawn around her.

Sato approached the photograph.

His expression changed.

"Who is she?"

Aizwa shook her head.

"I don't know."

Sato carefully picked up the photograph.

On the back was a single word.
