Rei's Apartment

Now — Rei's Apartment

Akane stared at Sato.

"Why are you smiling?"

Sato looked around the apartment.

"Because we got played."

Akane frowned.

"How?"

Sato pointed toward the evidence files.

"Casper Voss."

"What about him?"

"He isn't who he claims to be."

Akane's expression changed.

"What?"

"His identity is fake. We need to find out who he really was."

Akane immediately reached for her notebook.

"I'll look into it."

"And Rei?"

Sato's expression became serious again.

"We need his client list, phone records, financial activity — anything that tells us where he's been and who he's connected to."

Akane nodded.

"I'm on it."

She paused.

"But there's a problem."

"What?"

"We're trying to investigate an entire organization with only two detectives."

Sato sighed.

"Yeah…"

He looked toward the apartment walls, still covered with the symbol.

"We need more people."

Akane nodded.

"Then let's move before whoever is behind this gets another step ahead of us."
