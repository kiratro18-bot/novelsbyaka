University Festival: Day Two

After the drama ended yesterday, everything felt… unfinished.

POV: Noa, Haruki, Ren & Narrator Festival Grounds By AKA

POV: Noa

After the drama ended yesterday, everything felt… unfinished.

We clapped.

We smiled.

Then we went home.

No explanations.

No late-night messages.

Nothing.

Now it was the last day of the festival, and I stood in front of the mirror, fixing my hair for the third time.

I want to be with him today.

With Haruki.

Yesterday, he barely spoke to me.

Not angry.

Not cold.

Just… distant.

Why?

Was it the play?

Was it Sora?

Or was I the one misunderstanding everything?

I grabbed my bag and stepped out.

I won't avoid him today.

Scene: Haruki's Home Staring at the ceiling

POV: Haruki

I lay on my bed, staring at the ceiling.

I felt dead.

Empty in a way that didn't hurt loudly — just drained.

Yesterday replayed in my head.

The stage.

The tree.

The words.

Why did it have to be a tree?

I let out a breath.

"Noa…" I muttered.

What were my true feelings for her?

And why did Sora's face still appear when I closed my eyes?

Did I leave something unfinished… or am I just scared to move forward?

I sat up abruptly.

"Shut up," I told myself.

The festival wouldn't wait.

Neither would the answers.

POV: Ren Outside Sora's Home
🚪
Scene: Outside Sora's Home Ringing the bell

POV: Ren

Why am I here?

That question echoed in my head as I stood in front of her house.

Still, my hand reached forward.

I rang the bell.

The door opened.

There she was.

"Sora."

She blinked in surprise.

"Oh—Ren. Hi. You're here?"

"Yeah," I said, scratching the back of my neck.

"Um… will you come with me?"

She tilted her head. "Where?"

"To the university festival," I said.

"It's the last day."

For a second, I thought she might say no.

Then—

"Oh," she smiled softly. "Yeah. I will."

Something warm spread through my chest.

"Okay," I said, trying not to sound too happy.

"Let's go then."

We walked side by side, not touching, not rushing.

But for the first time in a while—

I was glad I asked.

I Have Let Go

Whatever I felt for Sora — I had finally put it down.

POV: Haruki & Noa Festival Grounds By AKA

POV: Haruki

My phone buzzed.

Noa: I'll be waiting for you at the university festival.

I stared at the screen longer than I should have.

Noa…

She'd been by my side since high school.

She noticed things. She cared. She stayed.

I know that.

Haruki: I'll be there.

I slipped my phone into my pocket and started walking.

That's when I saw them.

Near the university entrance.

Ren and Sora.

Standing close. Talking quietly. Not laughing — just… comfortable.

So that's how it is.

Sora talking to Ren.

Ren, the boy she loved.

Of course.

I figured it out too late, I thought.

I was never the one she was looking for.

My chest tightened, but I didn't stop walking.

"Shut up, Haruki," I muttered under my breath.

"You knew this would happen."

And for the first time, it didn't feel like a lie I was forcing myself to believe.

Just Say It
No running.
🌸 The Petal That Falls With a Smile Vol. 3 ·
POV: Noa The Confession

POV: Noa

Today.

Today is the day.

I was going to confess to Haruki during the fireworks.

My hands felt cold.

My heart wouldn't slow down.

Just say it, I told myself.

No running.

Then I saw him.

"Oh—Haruki!"

He turned.

"Hello, Noa."

"You look… in a good mood," I said, unsure.

He paused for a second.

Then smiled — not wide, not fake.

"Yeah," he said.

"I'm actually feeling better now."

Something about that smile felt different.

Calmer.

Resolved.

I didn't know why…

But a strange fear flickered in my chest.
