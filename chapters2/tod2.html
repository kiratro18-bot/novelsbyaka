Ch. 02

Wednesday

the rain kept its appointment.

The first thing I noticed that morning was that Haru was asleep.

Not metaphorically.

Actually asleep.

His head was resting on his folded arms, and he hadn't moved since I'd walked into the classroom.

Kenta dropped his bag onto the chair beside me.

"He's back for one day and already sleeping."

I looked at Haru.

"Maybe he's tired."

"Maybe he's been training for this."

"For what?"

"Professional sleeping."

I laughed.

Haru opened one eye.

"I can hear you."

Kenta froze.

"You're awake?"

"No."

He closed his eye again.

Kenta looked at me.

"See?"

I shook my head.

I put my bag down and took out the notebook.

It was still there.

I hadn't written anything in it.

I hadn't even touched the pages after last night.

I told myself I wasn't going to.

I opened it anyway.

Wednesday.

Someone will forget their umbrella.

I stared at the sentence.

Outside, the sky was cloudy.

Not rainy.

Just gray.

I checked the weather on my phone.

No rain expected.

"Looks like you're wrong," Kenta said.

I nearly dropped the notebook.

"What?"

He pointed at the window.

"You're staring at the sky like it personally offended you."

"Oh."

I closed the notebook.

"Nothing."

"You're weird."

"Thanks."

"You're welcome."

The morning passed normally.

That was probably the worst part.

Nothing happened.

Math was boring.

English was worse.

During the break, Kenta spent ten minutes trying to convince everyone that he'd seen a cat wearing a tiny backpack on his way to school.

Nobody believed him.

"I swear."

"You said it had glasses too," Mina said.

"It did."

"Why would a cat need glasses?"

"How am I supposed to know?"

"Maybe it was going to school."

"Exactly."

She stared at him.

"You realize that makes it less believable, right?"

Kenta looked offended.

I laughed.

For a while, I forgot about the notebook.

Then lunch came.

Still nothing.

The sky outside had cleared up.

I looked at the notebook again.

Nothing.

Maybe I'd been stupid.

Obviously I'd been stupid.

A sentence written in a random notebook couldn't predict something as ordinary as someone forgetting an umbrella.

There was no reason to believe that.

I felt relieved.

Which was strange.

I didn't realize how much I had been waiting for something to happen until I realized I didn't want anything to happen.

After lunch, we had PE.

We played basketball.

I was terrible.

Not normal terrible.

Embarrassing terrible.

At one point I tried to pass the ball and accidentally threw it directly at Kenta's face.

He caught it.

Barely.

"You trying to kill me?"

"Sorry."

"You looked me dead in the eyes."

"I panicked."

"You panicked for three seconds?"

"Long three seconds."

He threw the ball back.

It hit my shoulder.

"Ow."

"Justice."

After class, everyone went back to change.

That's when I noticed Haru standing near the windows.

He wasn't changing.

He was looking outside.

I walked over.

"You okay?"

He turned.

"Yeah."

"You've been quiet."

"I'm always quiet."

"Not really."

He smiled.

"Maybe you're just noticing me more."

I didn't know what to say to that.

So I said the first thing that came into my head.

"Your hair looks weird."

He touched his hair.

"What?"

"I don't know."

"Why would you say that?"

"Because you made it awkward."

"You made it awkward."

"Yeah."

He laughed.

Then he went back to his desk.

I looked outside.

The clouds had returned.

A few drops of rain hit the window.

I stared.

Then another.

Then another.

"No way."

Kenta walked past me.

"What?"

"Nothing."

The rain started getting heavier.

Everyone rushed toward the windows.

Someone groaned.

"I didn't bring an umbrella."

Another person said, "Same."

I looked at the notebook.

I hadn't even realized I was holding it.

My fingers felt cold.

I opened it.

The sentence was still there.

Wednesday.

Someone will forget their umbrella.

A few seconds later, Mina walked into the classroom.

She stopped near the door.

"Oh, come on."

I looked at her.

She looked outside.

Then at her empty hands.

"I forgot my umbrella."

Nobody reacted.

It wasn't exactly strange.

It was raining.

People forget umbrellas.

That's what umbrellas are famous for.

But I couldn't move.

Kenta looked at me.

"Why are you making that face?"

"What face?"

"That face."

"I don't have a face."

"Everyone has a face."

"Shut up."

He laughed.

I closed the notebook.

Maybe it was still coincidence.

It had to be.

I kept telling myself that.

Even when I walked home in the rain.

Even when I noticed that Haru had left school before everyone else.

Even when I passed the convenience store and remembered what I'd thought the night before.

I didn't think about the notebook much that evening.

I tried not to.

I played games.

Watched half an episode of something.

Forgot to do my homework.

Then remembered at eleven.

Normal stuff.

At around midnight, I opened the notebook again.

I don't know why.

Maybe curiosity.

Maybe boredom.

I flipped past Wednesday.

The next page had a date.

Under it was a sentence.

I read it once.

Then again.

Then a third time.

Thursday.

Someone will ask me about this notebook.

I stared at the page.

There was no name.

No explanation.

Just that sentence.

I looked around my room.

Nothing had changed.

My phone buzzed.

A message from Kenta.

"u doing homework?"

I typed:

"no"

Three dots appeared.

Then disappeared.

Then appeared again.

"same"

I smiled.

I almost closed the notebook.

Then I noticed something.

The handwriting on the Thursday page looked slightly different.

Not enough to be obvious.

Just enough that I noticed.

I turned back to Wednesday.

Then Tuesday.

Same handwriting.

Mostly.

I ran my finger over the ink.

I couldn't explain what bothered me about it.

Maybe nothing.

Maybe I was starting to look for things that weren't there.

I closed the notebook.

Tomorrow, someone would ask me about it.

That was what it said.

I went to sleep thinking about who it could be.

I had three possibilities.

By morning, I would have four.
