Prequel — The Petal That Falls With a Smile

The Confession

Chapter 3  POV: Sora

Spring arrived quietly.

One morning, I walked through the school gate and realized the snow was gone.

The mountains were still white in the distance, but the streets had begun to change.

And then I saw it.

The old tree.

The one Ren and I had visited all winter.

Pink.

Every branch was covered in sakura blossoms.

I stopped walking.

“Wow…”

“It finally bloomed.”

I turned.

Ren was standing behind me, his school bag hanging from one shoulder.

“You're late,” I said.

“You stopped walking.”

“Because look at it.”

“I know.”

He walked over and stood beside me.

For a while, neither of us said anything.

The tree was beautiful.

But somehow, it felt different from the last time we'd stood there.

Maybe because spring meant the school year was changing.

Maybe because we were getting older.

Or maybe because something about Ren had changed.

He had been acting strange lately.

Quieter.

More nervous.

Sometimes I'd catch him looking at me, and the moment I looked back, he'd turn away.

“Ren?”

“Hmm?”

“Is something wrong?”

“No.”

“You're lying.”

“I am not.”

“You are.”

He laughed awkwardly.

“I'll tell you later.”

“Later?”

“Yeah.”

I frowned.

“What does that mean?”

“You'll see.”

He walked away before I could ask anything else.

I watched him disappear through the school entrance.

“…What was that?”

After school, I went back to the tree.

I didn't know why.

Maybe I was waiting for Ren.

Maybe I just wanted to see the blossoms again.

I sat beneath the tree and opened my notebook.

A few minutes passed.

Then I heard footsteps.

“Sora.”

I looked up.

Ren stood there.

His face was red.

Not from the cold.

There wasn't much cold left.

“What's wrong?” I asked.

“Nothing.”

“You look nervous.”

“I am.”

That surprised me.

Ren sat beside me.

For a while, he stared at the ground.

Then he took a deep breath.

“Sora.”

“Yeah?”

“I've wanted to tell you something for a long time.”

My heart began beating faster.

“Okay…”

He looked at me.

His expression was completely different from usual.

Serious.

Nervous.

But determined.

“I like you.”

Everything stopped.

The wind.

The birds.

Even my thoughts.

I stared at him.

“…What?”

“I like you, Sora.”

He looked away almost immediately.

“I don't know when it happened. Maybe it was slowly. Maybe it was always there and I just didn't realize it.”

His hands tightened around his knees.

“But whenever something happens, you're the first person I want to tell.”

He laughed nervously.

“And when you're sad, I want to make you smile.”

I couldn't speak.

“I know we're still young,” he continued. “And I don't know what happens when we get older.”

He looked back at me.

“But I wanted you to know.”

My heart was pounding.

I liked Ren.

Of course I did.

He was my best friend.

The person who always stayed beside me.

The person who knew when I was pretending to be okay.

The person I looked for whenever I entered a room.

But hearing him say those words…

made everything feel different.

“Ren…”

“Yeah?”

I smiled.

“I like you too.”

His eyes widened.

“Really?”

I nodded.

“Really.”

For a second, he just stared at me.

Then he smiled.

The biggest smile I'd ever seen from him.

I laughed.

“Why are you smiling like that?”

“Because you said yes.”

“I didn't exactly say—”

“You said you liked me.”

“That doesn't automatically mean—”

“It does to me.”

I laughed again.

“Idiot.”

“Maybe.”

The wind passed through the branches.

A few petals drifted down between us.

One landed on my notebook.

I picked it up carefully.

“Ren.”

“Yeah?”

“Let's make a promise.”

He looked at me.

“No matter what happens…”

I held the petal between my fingers.

“…let's stay together.”

He smiled.

“Promise.”

Our hands met briefly as we bumped our fists together.

Neither of us knew how fragile promises could be.

Neither of us knew how quickly life could change.

For that moment, though—

I was happy.

Ren was happy.

And the sakura tree was blooming above us.

End of Chapter 3
