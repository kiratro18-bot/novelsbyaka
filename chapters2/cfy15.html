🔍 Case File: You · Chapter 15

Final Verdict — Part 3

Rain on Oak Street, a chair in an empty room, and a name that finally answers. Sato gets his confession — but not the one he was looking for.

Mystery Investigation Dark By AKA

Rain.

Again.

Sato stood beneath the streetlight, staring at the building in front of him.

Apartment 307.

The same address that had appeared in Envy's message.

The same place where Lust had disappeared.

Behind him, police vehicles stopped one after another.

Akane stepped out first.

Then Aizwa.

"Everyone's in position," Akane said.

Sato nodded.

His eyes remained on the building.

"You're sure he's inside?"

Sato looked at the photograph in his hand.

Envy had sent it.

A picture of Rei.

Or rather—

the man everyone knew as Rei.

"I'm sure."

Aizwa looked at him.

"You still think he's connected to the murders?"

Sato folded the photograph.

"I don't think."

He looked toward the dark windows.

"I know."

• • •

The door to Apartment 307 was open.

Sato entered first.

The room was empty.

No furniture.

No decorations.

Nothing.

Except for a chair in the center.

And a man sitting on it.

His hands were behind his back.

His head was lowered.

Sato stopped.

"Rei."

The man slowly lifted his head.

He smiled.

"You finally found me."

Akane raised her weapon.

"Don't move."

Rei looked at her.

Then at Aizwa.

Then back at Sato.

"You brought everyone."

Sato stepped forward.

"It's over."

Rei laughed.

"No."

His smile widened.

"You've only found me."

Sato's eyes narrowed.

"You're Lust."

The smile disappeared.

For the first time, Rei said nothing.

Sato continued.

"Ruby Zeni."

No response.

"Casper Voss."

Silence.

"Mia."

Nothing.

"Charlotte."

Rei finally looked down.

Sato watched him carefully.

"Those weren't random victims."

Rei slowly raised his head.

"You really want to know?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

Sato didn't hesitate.

"Because I'm a detective."

Rei laughed again.

"No."

He leaned back in the chair.

"That's not why."

Sato stared at him.

Rei's eyes sharpened.

"You're not chasing the truth because you're a detective."

"You're chasing it because you need it."

Silence.

Akane looked at Sato.

Sato didn't react.

Rei smiled.

"That's your desire, isn't it?"

Sato stepped closer.

"What's your real name?"

The smile vanished.

A long silence followed.

Then—

"Vaneiro."

Akane's eyes widened.

"Vaneiro?"

Rei nodded.

"Rei was never my name."

Sato stared at him.

"Then why use it?"

"Because names are useful."

Identity Confirmed
Vaneiro
Formerly Known As Rei
Codename Lust
Affiliation The 8

Vaneiro looked toward the window.

"People believe what they're given."

Sato thought about the case.

The DNA.

The alibis.

The symbols.

The victims.

Everything.

"You were working for Sengoku."

Vaneiro smiled faintly.

"Sengoku-sama."

Sato's expression hardened.

"Where is he?"

Vaneiro looked at him.

Then laughed.

"You still believe that's the question?"

Sato grabbed the back of the chair.

"Where is Sengoku?"

Vaneiro's eyes met his.

"Dead."

Everyone went silent.

Sato's grip tightened.

"You expect me to believe that?"

"I didn't say you should."

Vaneiro leaned forward.

"Sengoku-sama died."

"Then who has been giving you orders?"

Vaneiro smiled.

"That…"

He paused.

"Is something you haven't earned the answer to."

• • •

A sudden noise came from outside.

Akane turned.

"Movement!"

Sato looked toward the window.

A black figure stood across the street.

The same figure.

The one he'd seen months ago.

Sato ran.

By the time he reached the street—

nothing.

Only rain.

He looked around.

Empty.

Then he heard something behind him.

Vaneiro.

"Detective."

Sato turned.

Vaneiro was standing at the apartment entrance, surrounded by officers.

His eyes met Sato's.

"You should've asked about Envy."

Sato froze.

"Where is Envy?"

Vaneiro smiled.

"Gone."

The officers took him away.

Sato watched.

For the first time since the case began—

he felt like he had actually caught someone.

But it didn't feel like victory.

Three Days Later

The courtroom was silent.

Vaneiro stood before the judge.

His crimes had been linked to multiple murders.

The evidence was overwhelming.

The symbol.

The DNA.

The witness statements.

The connection to The 8.

And the confession he had given during interrogation.

Sato sat in the back.

Akane beside him.

Aizwa stood near the entrance.

The verdict was delivered.

Guilty.

Vaneiro showed no emotion.

The judge announced the sentence.

Death.

Akane looked at Sato.

"You okay?"

Sato didn't answer immediately.

He looked at Vaneiro.

Vaneiro was staring directly at him.

Then he smiled.

Not proudly.

Not happily.

Almost sadly.

As if he already knew something Sato didn't.

The Final Conversation

Later that evening, Sato was allowed one final conversation with Vaneiro.

A glass wall separated them.

Vaneiro sat on one side.

Sato on the other.

For several seconds, neither spoke.

Then Sato asked:

"Why did you kill them?"

Vaneiro looked down.

"Because I was Lust."

Sato frowned.

"That isn't an answer."

"It is."

Vaneiro raised his eyes.

"I wanted something."

"What?"

"More."

Sato remained silent.

Vaneiro continued.

"More attention. More pleasure. More control. More everything."

He smiled bitterly.

"And eventually, wanting wasn't enough."

Sato watched him.

"Did Sengoku make you like this?"

Vaneiro shook his head.

"No."

"Then why follow him?"

Vaneiro's expression became strangely calm.

"Because he understood something."

"What?"

"That everyone has a desire."

Sato's eyes narrowed.

Vaneiro leaned toward the glass.

"Even you."

Sato said nothing.

Vaneiro smiled.

"Especially you."

The guard entered.

"Time."

Sato stood.

Before leaving, he asked one final question.

"Did Envy betray you?"

Vaneiro looked toward the floor.

Then laughed quietly.

"Yes."

"Why?"

Vaneiro looked back at him.

"Ask Envy."

The door opened.

Sato stopped.

Vaneiro called after him.

"Sato."

He turned.

Vaneiro's smile was gone.

"Don't find Sengoku."

Sato stared at him.

"Why?"

Vaneiro answered quietly.

"Because if you do…"

A pause.

"…he'll find you first."

6:03 AM

The execution was carried out.

No cameras.

No crowd.

No final speech.

Only silence.

Vaneiro closed his eyes.

For a moment, he remembered the first time Sengoku had spoken to him.

The first time he heard the word—

desire.

Then everything went quiet.

6:30 AM

Sato stood outside.

Rain tapped against the window.

Akane approached.

"It's done."

Sato nodded.

"Vaneiro?"

"Dead."

Sato looked down at the case file in his hands.

📁
Case File: You Victims · Symbols · The 8 · Sengoku · Vaneiro · Envy

He opened it.

Inside were photographs.

Then something slipped from between the pages.

A small piece of paper.

Sato picked it up.

There was only one sentence.

His face went completely still.

📝 Note

"One desire has fallen."
Underneath it — eight words.
"Seven remain. We will see you soon."

Akane looked over his shoulder.

"What is it?"

Sato didn't answer.

He folded the paper.

Far away, in an unknown room—

someone placed a photograph on a table.

Vaneiro.

A red line was drawn across his face.

Then another photograph was placed beside it.

Sato.

Someone picked up a pen.

And wrote:

✎ Written

DESIRE: TRUTH

The pen stopped.

A voice whispered:

The light went out.
