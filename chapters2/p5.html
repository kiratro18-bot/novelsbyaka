Prequel — The Petal That Falls With a Smile

Before I Leave

Chapter 5  POV: Sora

The morning came too quickly.

I stood in front of my mirror, staring at my school uniform.

Today was my last day.

My suitcase was already packed.

My new school uniform was waiting at home.

Everything had been decided.

Everything except one thing.

Ren.

I picked up my phone.

I typed:

Ren, can we talk after school?

I stared at the message.

Then deleted it.

I typed again.

I have something important to tell you.

Deleted.

Again.

Meet me at the tree.

My finger hovered over the screen.

Then I locked my phone.

I couldn't do it.

School felt strange that day.

Every ordinary thing suddenly felt important.

The morning bell.

The teacher's voice.

The conversations around me.

Even the squeak of chalk against the board.

I looked at Ren several times.

He noticed.

"What?"

"Nothing."

"You keep looking at me."

"I do not."

"You do."

I smiled.

"Maybe you're imagining it."

He laughed.

"You're weird today."

I'm sorry.

I wanted to say it.

But I didn't.

After school, I went to the sakura tree.

Most of the petals had already fallen.

Only a few remained.

Ren arrived a few minutes later.

"There you are."

"Yeah."

He sat beside me.

"Tomorrow's the weekend."

I nodded.

"Yeah."

"Want to come here again?"

My chest tightened.

"Ren…"

"Hmm?"

I looked at him.

He was smiling.

The same smile I'd seen every day.

The same boy who had promised he would stay.

I wanted to tell him everything.

I wanted to tell him I was leaving.

I wanted him to stop me.

But I was afraid.

Afraid that he'd ask me to stay.

Afraid that I'd say yes.

Afraid that our promise would become impossible to keep.

So I did the only thing I thought I could do.

I lied.

"I think…"

I looked down.

"…we shouldn't see each other anymore."

His smile disappeared.

"What?"

"I think we should stop."

"Why?"

I couldn't answer.

"Did I do something?"

"No."

"Then why?"

My eyes burned.

"Because…"

I forced myself to look at him.

"I don't feel the same way anymore."

The words hurt more coming out than I expected.

Ren stared at me.

For several seconds, he said nothing.

Then he smiled faintly.

It wasn't his real smile.

"Oh."

He looked down.

"I understand."

No.

You don't.

I wanted to scream it.

You don't understand.

I'm leaving tomorrow.

I still like you.

I'm sorry.

But I said nothing.

Ren stood up.

"Then… I guess I'll see you around."

He turned away.

"Ren."

He stopped.

I almost called him back.

Almost.

But fear won.

"...Never mind."

He nodded.

Then walked away.

I watched him disappear.

Only after he was gone did I finally let the tears fall.

I pressed my hands against my face.

"I lied…"

The sakura tree stood silently above me.

A single petal drifted down.

I caught it.

Tomorrow, I'd be gone.

And Ren would remember something that wasn't true.

That I didn't love him.

That I had rejected him.

That I had pushed him away.

Maybe that would make leaving easier for him.

At least…

that's what I told myself.

The next morning.

The car waited outside.

My bags were in the trunk.

I looked back at the house one last time.

Then at the road.

Somewhere beyond those streets was the school.

The hill.

The sakura tree.

Ren.

I wanted to run back.

But I didn't.

The car started moving.

I watched my hometown disappear through the window.

And somewhere behind me—

I left the boy I loved without telling him the truth.

Years later, when someone asked me about the first person I ever loved, I would remember this day.

And I wouldn't tell them the whole story.

I'd simply say—

"I pushed a boy away."

Because saying I loved him, but I was too afraid to stay…

was harder.

Much harder.

The last sakura petal disappeared from my hand.

And my hometown faded into the distance.

End of Prequel
