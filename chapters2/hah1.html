Chapter 1 — Who Is He?

Sayori's POV

I was talking with my friends.

Aoi and Rika.

I was really happy that Aoi was in the same classroom as me. Rika too.

Third year of high school.

The final year.

There are still many things I don't remember clearly. My parents told me that I had an accident that day.

But the details are still blurry.

Sometimes it feels like there are memories just outside my reach.

Then suddenly a boy entered the classroom.

I looked at him.

Hmm… that boy.

I feel like I have seen him somewhere before.

But where?

He quietly walked to the back and sat in the last seat.

When I looked at him again, something felt strange.

My chest felt tight.

A feeling I couldn't explain.

What is this feeling…?

Rika's POV

Kazuki still hasn't talked to her.

And Sayori… she's zoning out again like always.

I sighed.

What should I do?

If they keep acting like this, nothing will ever happen.

Then an idea came to my mind.

Yeah.

Lunch time.

That's when I'll make them talk.
