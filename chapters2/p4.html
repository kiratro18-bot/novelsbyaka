Prequel — The Petal That Falls With a Smile

The Last Week

Chapter 4  POV: Sora

Something felt different.

I noticed it the moment I got home.

Mom was sitting at the dining table.

There were papers in front of her.

She looked up when I entered.

“Sora, come sit down.”

I stopped.

“Why?”

“I need to tell you something.”

Her voice was gentle.

Too gentle.

I slowly sat across from her.

She pushed one of the papers toward me.

I looked down.

I didn't understand it at first.

Then I saw the words.

Transfer Application.

My fingers went cold.

“What's this?”

Mom hesitated.

“Your father's work is being transferred.”

I stared at her.

“Where?”

“Far away.”

The room suddenly felt smaller.

“How far?”

“Far enough that you'll need to change schools.”

I didn't answer.

My eyes stayed on the paper.

“When?”

“Next week.”

Next week.

The words repeated in my head.

Next week.

Next week.

I thought about the classroom.

The hill.

The snow.

The sakura tree.

Ren.

My chest tightened.

“Do I have to go?”

Mom looked at me sadly.

“We don't really have a choice.”

I lowered my head.

“Okay.”

That was all I could say.

The next morning, Ren was waiting outside the school gate.

He smiled when he saw me.

“Morning.”

“Morning.”

“You look tired.”

“I didn't sleep much.”

“Why?”

I looked at him.

For a moment, I almost told him.

I'm leaving.

The words reached my throat.

But they wouldn't come out.

“Just… couldn't sleep.”

Ren frowned.

“You're not sick, are you?”

“No.”

“Promise?”

“Promise.”

He smiled.

“Good.”

We walked toward the classroom together.

I kept looking at him.

The way he laughed.

The way he complained about homework.

The way he always walked slightly slower whenever I fell behind.

I tried to memorize everything.

After school, we went to the sakura tree.

The petals had started falling.

Ren sat beside me.

“Summer's coming soon.”

“Yeah.”

“Then no more freezing mornings.”

“Good.”

He smiled.

I looked up at the branches.

“Ren.”

“Hmm?”

“What if…”

I stopped.

“What if what?”

I looked away.

“What if someone had to leave?”

He thought about it.

“Like moving?”

“Yeah.”

“I'd be sad.”

His answer came immediately.

“Would you stay in contact?”

“I'd try.”

“What if they couldn't?”

He frowned.

“Then I'd find a way.”

I looked at him.

“You'd really do that?”

“Of course.”

He smiled.

“Why are you asking weird questions?”

I laughed quietly.

“Just wondering.”

He leaned back against the tree.

“Anyway, you're not going anywhere.”

My smile froze.

“Why?”

“Because you promised.”

My heart hurt.

“I did?”

“You said we'd stay together.”

He looked at me.

“So you can't break it.”

I smiled.

“Yeah.”

I couldn't tell him.

Not yet.

Maybe tomorrow.

Maybe the day after.

There was still time.

Three days later.

I still hadn't told him.

Two days left.

Still nothing.

One day left.

I stood outside the school after everyone had gone home.

Ren was waiting for me.

“Sora?”

I turned.

He smiled.

“Ready to go?”

I looked at him.

Tomorrow, I would leave.

Tomorrow, this place would become a memory.

And Ren—

He would become someone I could only remember.

“Yeah,” I whispered.

“Let's go.”

We walked home together.

I kept thinking about telling him.

But every time I opened my mouth, fear stopped me.

What if he cried?

What if he got angry?

What if he asked me to stay?

What if I couldn't leave after hearing him say it?

So I said nothing.

And that silence became the first mistake I couldn't take back.

That evening, I packed my bags.

Before going to sleep, I opened my notebook.

A single sakura petal was pressed between the pages.

I touched it gently.

“I'm sorry, Amamiya Ren.”

The room was quiet.

And for the first time—

I cried.

End of Chapter 4
