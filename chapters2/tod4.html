Ch. 04

The Seat by the Window

don't sit by the window.

I didn't sleep very well that night.

Not because I was scared.

At least, I don't think I was.

I just kept waking up for no reason.

At 2:13, I checked my phone.

Nothing.

At 3:41, I checked again.

Still nothing.

At 5:06, I gave up and stared at the ceiling.

The sentence in the notebook kept coming back to me.

You already know why.

I didn't know why.

That was the problem.

I finally got out of bed at 6:30.

My first thought was that I should throw the notebook away.

My second thought was that throwing away a mysterious notebook was exactly what someone in a horror movie would do right before everything got worse.

So I didn't.

I put it in my bag.

At school, everything looked normal.

Which somehow made it worse.

Kenta was complaining about the cafeteria.

Mina was trying to finish her homework before class.

Someone near the windows was arguing about a football match.

Haru was sitting in his usual seat.

Nothing strange.

I almost felt embarrassed about yesterday.

Maybe I'd made everything bigger in my head.

I sat down.

Kenta leaned over.

"You look terrible."

"Thanks."

"Did you sleep?"

"Not really."

"Studying?"

"No."

"Gaming?"

"No."

"Thinking about something?"

I looked at him.

He smiled.

"Gotcha."

"Shut up."

He laughed.

The bell rang.

Our homeroom teacher walked in.

"Before we start, there's been a change in seating."

Everyone groaned.

A few people immediately started complaining.

"Why?"

"Because you complained last time," the teacher said.

That shut them up.

She held a sheet of paper.

"Some of you will be moving."

I didn't care.

Until she read my name.

"You're moving to the back."

I looked up.

"Me?"

"Yes."

"Why?"

"Because I'm the teacher."

Fair enough.

I picked up my things.

My new seat was near the window.

Two rows behind Haru.

I sat down.

The desk was old.

There were scratches all over it.

Someone had carved something into the corner.

I leaned closer.

At first I couldn't read it.

Then I saw the letters.

R.

Just one letter.

I rubbed my thumb over it.

"Ren?"

I whispered the name without thinking.

Haru turned around.

"What?"

"Nothing."

He looked at the desk.

Then at me.

His expression changed.

Only for a second.

But I noticed.

"You shouldn't sit there."

I laughed.

"Why?"

He didn't answer.

"Haru?"

"It's nothing."

He turned back around.

I stared at him.

That was getting annoying.

Everyone kept saying things were nothing.

I took out my notebook.

The normal one.

Not the black one.

I didn't want to look at the black notebook during class.

For some reason, the idea of opening it while sitting at that desk made me uncomfortable.

The first lesson went normally.

Then the second.

During the break, I asked Kenta about the desk.

"Do you know who used to sit here?"

He looked at it.

"No."

"Someone named Ren?"

Kenta thought for a moment.

"Ren?"

"Shinoda Ren."

His expression changed.

Not much.

But enough.

"You know him?"

"No."

"You hesitated."

"I was thinking."

"About?"

"Whether I know him."

"You don't?"

"I don't think so."

I stared at him.

Kenta sighed.

"Okay, I've heard the name."

"From where?"

"I don't remember."

"That's convenient."

"I'm serious."

He rubbed the back of his neck.

"I think someone mentioned him last year."

"Who?"

"I don't know."

He walked away.

I watched him leave.

For once, he wasn't joking.

At lunch, I stayed behind.

Everyone else went to the cafeteria.

I took the black notebook out.

I opened it.

Nothing new.

I turned through every page.

Tuesday.

Wednesday.

Thursday.

Then the page without a date.

You already know why.

I flipped it over.

Blank.

I checked the back cover.

Nothing.

Then I noticed something.

The inside of the back cover had a small piece of paper stuck to it.

I carefully pulled it out.

It was folded.

I opened it.

There was a classroom number written on it.

3-B.

I stared at it.

Our classroom was 3-B.

Underneath the number was another line.

Don't sit by the window.

I looked at my seat.

The window was right beside me.

I felt a strange pressure in my chest.

Not fear.

Something closer to recognition.

Like I'd seen this before.

But I couldn't remember when.

I stood up.

The classroom was empty.

I walked toward the window.

Outside, the school courtyard was almost completely empty.

A few students were walking toward the cafeteria.

Nothing unusual.

Then I saw someone standing near the old building.

A girl.

She was looking toward our classroom.

I couldn't see her face clearly.

She lifted her hand.

For a second, I thought she was waving.

Then she turned around and walked away.

I opened the window.

"Hey!"

She didn't stop.

I leaned farther out.

"Wait!"

Nothing.

I almost climbed onto the ledge.

Then I stopped myself.

That would have been stupid.

I closed the window.

When I turned around, Haru was standing in the doorway.

I jumped.

"Jesus."

He frowned.

"Sorry."

"You walk too quietly."

"You were shouting."

"I wasn't shouting."

"You were."

I looked at him.

Then at the black notebook in my hand.

His eyes moved toward it.

"Give me that."

"No."

He stepped into the classroom.

"I'm serious."

"So am I."

"Why are you doing this?"

"Doing what?"

"Looking into things that don't concern you."

I stared at him.

"What happened to Ren?"

Haru went silent.

I had expected him to deny knowing the name.

Instead, he looked at the empty seat beside the window.

Then he said,

"He used to sit there."

I glanced at my desk.

"Here?"

"Yeah."

"What happened to him?"

"He disappeared."

I waited.

Haru looked at me.

"Not like that."

"What does that mean?"

"He just stopped coming."

"Transferred?"

"I don't know."

"You were his friend?"

"No."

"Then how do you know?"

Haru looked toward the window.

"Because I was the one sitting next to him."

I didn't know what to say.

He continued.

"One day he was here."

He pointed at the seat.

"The next day he wasn't."

"Nobody knew where he went?"

"They said he transferred."

"And?"

"And that was it."

I looked down at the notebook.

"Why does this have his name?"

Haru didn't answer.

I opened the notebook.

A page I'd never seen before was there.

I was sure it hadn't been there yesterday.

There was a date at the top.

Friday.

Underneath:

Someone will ask what happened to Ren.

I stared at it.

Tomorrow was Friday.

My fingers tightened around the page.

"Haru."

He didn't respond.

I looked up.

He was staring at the notebook.

Not surprised.

Not confused.

Just tired.

"You're not the first person to find it," he said.

"What?"

He walked toward the door.

"You're just the first person who kept reading."

Then he left.

I remained in the classroom.

The bell hadn't rung yet.

The room was completely quiet.

I looked at the empty seat beside mine.

Then at the window.

For some reason, I suddenly remembered something.

A desk.

A boy sitting beside the window.

Someone laughing.

And me saying—

"I'll see you tomorrow."

I blinked.

The memory disappeared.

I couldn't remember the boy's face.

I couldn't remember when it happened.

I couldn't even remember saying it.

But I knew one thing.

I had been sitting in this classroom before.

And someone had been sitting beside me.
