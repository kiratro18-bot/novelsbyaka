Prequel — The Petal That Falls With a Smile

The Boy Who Stayed

Chapter 2  POV: Sora

Winter in the north was long.

Not the kind of winter you saw in pictures.

The cold stayed in the streets long after the snow stopped falling. The mountains beyond town remained covered in white, and the mornings were always quiet.

I hated waking up early.

But I liked school.

Mostly because Ren was there.

“Sora!”

I stopped walking.

Ren was standing outside the school gate, waving at me.

“You're late.”

“I'm not late.”

“You are.”

I looked at the clock above the entrance.

“…I have two minutes.”

“That means you're late.”

“That makes no sense.”

He laughed.

We walked into the school together.

Middle school wasn't particularly exciting.

Classes.

Homework.

Tests.

Lunch.

Repeat.

But somehow, everything felt better when Ren was around.

He had always been like that.

Whenever I was quiet, he'd notice.

Whenever I was upset, he'd ask.

Whenever I said I was fine—

He knew I wasn't.

That afternoon, we stayed behind after school.

The snow had started falling again.

I stood beside the classroom window, watching it cover the schoolyard.

“Beautiful,” I whispered.

Ren looked outside.

“You really like snow.”

“Yeah.”

“Why?”

I thought about it.

“Because everything becomes quiet.”

He looked at me.

“That's a weird reason.”

“You're weird.”

“Hey.”

I laughed.

Then something outside caught my attention.

Near the far edge of the school grounds stood an old tree.

Its branches were bare.

Snow rested quietly on them.

“Ren.”

“Hmm?”

“What's that tree?”

He looked.

“Oh. That?”

“Yeah.”

“I think it's an old sakura tree.”

“Sakura?”

“Yeah. It blooms in spring.”

I stared at it.

“It doesn't look like it'll bloom.”

Ren shrugged.

“You have to wait.”

“For how long?”

“Until spring.”

I smiled.

“Then let's come back.”

Ren looked at me.

“Okay.”

We walked outside.

The snow crunched beneath our shoes as we approached the tree.

I placed my hand against its cold trunk.

“Do you think it'll really bloom?”

“Of course.”

“You sound very confident.”

“Because it always does.”

I looked at the branches again.

Bare.

Empty.

Almost lifeless.

But somehow…

I believed him.

“Ren?”

“Yeah?”

“When we're older…”

He waited.

“Do you think we'll still be friends?”

He stared at me like I'd asked the strangest question in the world.

“Obviously.”

“You don't know that.”

“I do.”

“How?”

He smiled.

“Because I'll stay.”

Those words were simple.

Almost meaningless.

At least, that's what I thought then.

I smiled back.

“Okay.”

We stood beneath the tree as snow slowly fell around us.

Neither of us knew how many times that tree would see us after that.

Neither of us knew how many promises would be made beneath it.

And neither of us knew that one day…

I'd be the one leaving.

For now, though—

I was happy.

And Ren was beside me.

That was enough.

End of Chapter 2
