Chapter 2 — A Boy

Sayori’s POV

Lunch break was about to start.

But Rika was nowhere to be found.

She told me she was going to the washroom, but she was taking a long time.

I was sitting at my desk when the teacher suddenly called my name.

“Sayori, could you take these printouts to the science lab?”

Then the teacher added,

“Take him with you.”

Him?

I turned around.

Oh… that boy.

The same one who entered the classroom earlier.

He walked up to me and looked at the stack of papers.

“Those look heavy,” he said.

“Let me carry them.”

Before I could say anything, he took the printouts from my hands.

“Lead the way.”

We walked toward the science lab together.

Something about this moment felt strange.

Not uncomfortable.

Just… familiar.

Like I already knew who he was.

When we reached the lab, he placed the printouts on the table.

Then he said quietly,

“Don’t try to push yourself too much.”

I looked at him.

Still confused.

But for some reason… I didn’t ask his name.

When I returned to the classroom, the lunch break had already started.

And that boy…

He was nowhere to be found.

End of Chapter 2
